Each person can connect their own calendars, and their meetings become time
to log — Consultainer suggests which project each one belongs to.

## Connecting a calendar

On your **Profile → Calendars**, paste the calendar's secret iCal (.ics)
link. Up to five per person.

- **Google Calendar** — Settings → your calendar → *Integrate calendar* →
  *Secret address in iCal format*.
- **Outlook / Microsoft 365** — Settings → Calendar → *Shared calendars* →
  *Publish a calendar* (can view all details) → ICS link.
- **Apple iCloud** — in Calendar, share the calendar as a *Public Calendar*
  and copy the `webcal://` link.

Anyone with that link can read the calendar, so it's stored encrypted and only
used to fetch your meetings. Only you see your meetings in Consultainer.

## Sorting meetings

Right after connecting, and then every hour, meetings from the last two weeks
that have ended show up under **Time → Meetings** (you're notified, at most
daily, when there are some to sort). Left out: all-day events, blocks marked
*free*, cancelled meetings, and meetings you declined. Meeting rooms don't
count as attendees.

Each meeting gets a suggested project, with the reason shown:

1. The project you logged earlier meetings in the **same recurring series**
   to.
2. A **client whose email domain matches the attendees** — taken from the
   client's contacts, billing email and website (public domains like
   gmail.com, and your own organization's domain, are ignored) — on the
   project you've logged to most for that client.
3. A **project or client named in the meeting title**.

For each meeting:

- **Log** — adds the meeting's length as a time entry on the chosen project,
  dated the meeting's day, described with its title; billable unless you
  untick **Billable**.
- **Ignore** — it isn't project work.
- **Always for this series** — sort the rest of a recurring meeting the same
  way: other unsorted occurrences now, and future ones automatically on each
  sync. **Forget them all** on the Calendars card clears these.

**Log all suggested** accepts every suggestion at once.

Over MCP: `list_meetings`, `log_meeting`, `ignore_meeting`.

## Self-hosting notes

Calendar links are fetched by your server, so Consultainer only connects to
public addresses: links to `localhost`, private networks, link-local / cloud
metadata addresses and the like are refused — including through redirects or
hostnames that resolve there. If your calendars are served from a private
network (e.g. Nextcloud on your LAN), set `ALLOW_PRIVATE_FETCH=true` in
`.env`. Syncing runs from the scheduler's hourly
`/api/cron/calendar-sync` call — see [Self-hosting](/self-hosting).
