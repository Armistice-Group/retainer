title: Signed agreements
description: Pull signed agreements from DocuSign, Documenso and Ironclad onto clients and projects
---

See every signed MSA, SOW and NDA where you work: on the client's page
("Acme MSA · Signed Mar 1, 2026 · Jane Buyer") and on the project it's for,
with a link to open it in the e-signature service and a stored copy of the
signed PDF.

Consultainer only reads from these services. It pulls agreements that are
fully signed (completed) and never creates, sends or changes anything there.

**Who can do what**

| | Owner | Admin | Member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Save the DocuSign app credentials for the instance | Yes | | |
| Connect or disconnect DocuSign, Documenso, Ironclad | Yes | Yes | |
| Link, unlink and dismiss agreements; **Sync now** | Yes | Yes | |
| See linked agreements on clients and projects | Yes | Yes | Yes, see [Who sees an agreement](#who-sees-an-agreement) |

Everything below happens in **Settings → Agreements**, a tab only owners and
admins see. **Settings → Integrations** has a **Signed agreements** card that
links to it.

## How it works

1. Every hour (and when you click **Sync now**), Consultainer asks each
   connected service for agreements completed since the last sync.
2. Each new agreement lands in the **Agreements inbox**, with its title,
   signed date, signers, and a suggested client when one matches (see
   [Suggested clients](#suggested-clients)). Its signed PDF is downloaded and
   stored with your other uploads.
3. An owner or admin links it to a client, and optionally one of that
   client's projects, or dismisses it.
4. Linked agreements show in a **Signed agreements** card on the client's
   page (including its projects' agreements, labelled) and on the project's
   page.

Syncing again never creates duplicates, and never undoes a link or a
dismissal. If the title or signers change in the service, the next sync
updates them.

## Connect DocuSign

DocuSign needs an app (an "integration key") registered once for the
instance by an **owner**; then an **owner or admin** connects the
organization's DocuSign account.

### Demo or production?

DocuSign has two separate environments:

- **Demo** (`account-d.docusign.com`, `appdemo.docusign.com`): free developer
  accounts. Use it to try the integration. It only sees demo envelopes.
- **Production** (`account.docusign.com`, `app.docusign.com`): your real
  account. DocuSign only lets an app use production after it passes
  DocuSign's go-live review.

Set **Environment** in Consultainer (or `DOCUSIGN_ENVIRONMENT`) to match.
If it isn't set, Consultainer uses demo.

### Register the app (owner, once)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the redirect URI">
    Open Consultainer at the address your team uses (not `localhost`), go to
    **Settings → Agreements** and open **How to set this up** on the
    **DocuSign** card. Step 4 shows the redirect URI with a **Copy** button,
    like `https://your-instance/api/integrations/docusign/callback`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the app in DocuSign">
    Sign in to **Apps and Keys**:
    [admindemo.docusign.com/apps-and-keys](https://admindemo.docusign.com/apps-and-keys)
    for demo (a free developer account from
    [developers.docusign.com](https://developers.docusign.com) works), or
    [admin.docusign.com/apps-and-keys](https://admin.docusign.com/apps-and-keys)
    for production. Click **Add App and Integration Key** and name it
    Consultainer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Authorization Code Grant and add a secret key">
    Under **Authentication**, answer **Yes** to "Is your application able to
    securely store a client secret?" (Authorization Code Grant). Click
    **Add Secret Key** and copy it now. DocuSign only shows it once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the redirect URI">
    Under **Additional settings → Redirect URIs**, click **Add URI**, paste
    the redirect URI from step 1, and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the credentials in Consultainer">
    In the DocuSign card's credentials form:

    | DocuSign | Consultainer field | Or in `.env` |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Integration Key | **Integration key** | `DOCUSIGN_CLIENT_ID` |
    | Secret key | **Secret key** | `DOCUSIGN_CLIENT_SECRET` |
    | Demo or production | **Environment** | `DOCUSIGN_ENVIRONMENT=demo` or `production` |

    Click **Save credentials**. Values set in `.env` win and show as
    read-only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Connect the account (owner or admin)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    On the DocuSign card, click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and allow access">
    Sign in to DocuSign and click **Allow access**. Consultainer asks for the
    `signature` scope (to read envelopes) and `extended` (so the connection
    keeps working without signing in again). You're sent back to
    **Settings → Agreements**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pull agreements">
    Click **Sync now**. The first sync looks back three years.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Consultainer uses the DocuSign account marked as your default (from
DocuSign's user info), and reads the completed envelopes the person who
connected can see in that account. Envelopes other users sent may not be
visible to them; connect with a DocuSign admin, or someone who sends your
agreements, if some are missing.

**Going to production:** in Apps and Keys, start the app's go-live review
(DocuSign requires a number of successful demo API calls first; a few syncs
cover it). Once approved, open the app in your production account, add a
secret key and the redirect URI there, switch **Environment** to
**Production** in Consultainer with the new secret key, and click
**Reconnect**.

## Connect Documenso

Documenso works with its cloud (`app.documenso.com`) or your own
self-hosted instance. It uses an API key, so there's no app to register.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an API token in Documenso">
    In Documenso, open the team whose documents you want and go to **Team
    settings → API tokens** (or **Settings → API tokens** for personal
    documents). Enter the name Consultainer, choose an expiry (or none), and
    click **Create token**. Copy it now; Documenso only shows it once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    In **Settings → Agreements → Documenso**, leave **Documenso URL** as
    `https://app.documenso.com`, or enter the address you open your own
    Documenso at. Paste the token as **API key** and click **Connect
    Documenso**.

    Consultainer reads one document to check the key works before saving it
    (encrypted). To change the URL or key later, fill the form in again and
    click **Update**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Documenso can't be asked for "completed since a date", so each sync reads
the list of completed documents (up to 5,000) and keeps the ones that
changed since the last sync. Consultainer uses Documenso's v2 `document`
API (`/api/v2/document`), which Documenso has marked as being replaced by
its newer envelope API but still serves; it's also what older self-hosted
versions have.

A self-hosted Documenso on a private network (a LAN address, `.local`,
`localhost`) is blocked unless the server sets `ALLOW_PRIVATE_FETCH=true`,
the same switch calendar links use.

## Connect Ironclad

Ironclad uses OAuth client credentials: an Ironclad admin registers a client
app in your Ironclad account, and Consultainer gets its own tokens with it.
Your Ironclad plan needs API access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register a client app in Ironclad">
    In Ironclad, register an OAuth client app for Consultainer that uses the
    **Client Credentials** grant, with the scopes
    `public.records.readRecords` and `public.records.readAttachments` only.
    Copy its client ID and client secret.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    In **Settings → Agreements → Ironclad**, pick the Ironclad site you sign
    in at (`ironcladapp.com`, `eu1.ironcladapp.com` or
    `demo.ironcladapp.com`), paste the client ID and secret, and enter the
    email of the Ironclad user Consultainer reads as. Ironclad answers API
    calls made with client credentials as that person (`x-as-user-email`),
    so Consultainer sees the records they can see. Click **Connect
    Ironclad**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every Ironclad record counts as a signed agreement (records are Ironclad's
executed-contract repository). Consultainer reads:

- the record's name as the title;
- the signed date from the record's `executedDate`, `signedDate` or
  `agreementDate` property, whichever it finds first;
- any email addresses in the record's properties as signers (Ironclad
  records don't have a standard signer list);
- `counterpartyName`, to suggest a client by name;
- the `signedCopy` attachment as the signed PDF.

<Note>
Ironclad's public API docs didn't let us confirm every detail. These parts
are best effort and may need adjusting for your account: the link
**Open in Ironclad** uses (`https://<site>/records/<record id>`), which
properties hold signer emails and the signed date (property names vary per
Ironclad account), and the `signedCopy` attachment key. If they don't fit
your account, agreements still arrive; link them by hand.
</Note>

## Check it worked

- Each connected service's card says **Connected** and, after a sync,
  **Last synced** with a date. A failed sync shows its error in red
  under it (see [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting)).
- The **Agreements inbox** lists completed agreements with **Open**, and
  **Signed copy** where the PDF was stored.
- After you link one, the client's page shows it under **Signed
  agreements**.

## Link agreements to clients and projects

In the **Agreements inbox**, each agreement has a client picker, already set
to the suggested client if there is one. Pick the client and, if the
agreement is for one project, the project (**Whole client** otherwise), and
click **Link**. Click **Dismiss** for anything that doesn't belong to a
client (an employment contract, a vendor agreement). Dismissed agreements are
listed under **Dismissed** at the bottom of the inbox, with **Back to
inbox** to undo; later syncs don't bring them back.

To move an agreement, unlink it (the unlink icon on the client or project
page) and link it again from the inbox. Deleting a client sends its
agreements back to the inbox; deleting a project keeps its agreements on the
client, client-wide.

### Paste a link instead

To link one specific agreement without waiting for the inbox, open the
client's or project's **Documents → Add**, choose **Link**, and paste the
agreement's address:

- DocuSign: `https://app.docusign.com/documents/details/<envelope id>` (or
  `appdemo.docusign.com`).
- Documenso: `https://app.documenso.com/t/<team>/documents/<id>`, or the same
  path on your self-hosted Documenso.
- Ironclad: any `ironcladapp.com` address with the record's id in it.

When an owner or admin adds it, Consultainer fetches that agreement through
the organization's connection and links it to the client (and project)
under **Signed agreements**, instead of adding a plain link. It must be
completed. For a member, the address is added as an ordinary linked
document.

### Suggested clients

Consultainer compares each signer's email address with every client's
email, billing email, contacts' emails and website:

- a signer whose email is one of a client's contacts counts most;
- a signer at the same company domain as a client's contact or website
  (`jane@acme.io` and `acme.io`, including subdomains) counts next;
- for Ironclad, a counterparty name that matches a client's name (ignoring
  Inc., LLC and the like) counts too.

Free email domains (`gmail.com`, `outlook.com` and similar) and your own
organization's domains (the organization domain and your members' email
domains) never count, since your own signature is on every agreement. When
two clients match equally well there's no suggestion. It's only a
suggestion: nothing is linked until you click **Link**.

## Who sees an agreement

- **Unlinked** agreements (the inbox) are only visible to owners and admins.
- **Client-wide** agreements are visible to everyone who can see the
  client, which is everyone in the organization.
- Agreements **linked to a project** follow the project: on a confidential
  project, only owners, admins and the people on that project see them, on
  the client page too.

**Open in DocuSign** (or Documenso, Ironclad) takes people to the agreement
in that service, so they need access there too. **Download signed copy**
serves the stored PDF from Consultainer, with the same visibility as above.
Opening or downloading is recorded in the audit log, and so are links,
unlinks and dismissals.

The [API](/api-reference/clients#list-signed-agreements-for-a-client)
(`GET /api/v1/clients/{id}/agreements`) and the MCP `list_agreements` tool
return the same agreements the key's user can see.

## The stored signed copy

For each completed agreement, Consultainer downloads the signed PDF once:

- DocuSign: all of the envelope's documents combined into one PDF (without
  the certificate of completion).
- Documenso: the signed version of the document.
- Ironclad: the record's signed copy.

It's stored where uploads are stored (the database, or your S3 bucket if
[object storage](/self-hosting) is set up). PDFs over 25 MB aren't stored;
those still open in the service.

If [filing](/integrations/files) is on with **Uploaded documents** ticked,
each linked agreement's PDF is also filed to
`<root>/<Client>/Agreements/<title>.pdf`. It's filed once, when it's linked
(or by the hourly filing catch-up); linking it to a different client later
doesn't move the filed copy.

## Disconnecting

**Disconnect** on a service's card (owner or admin) deletes the saved
tokens or key. Agreements already pulled stay linked where they are, with
their stored PDFs; new ones stop arriving. To revoke access on the
service's side as well, remove the app's access in DocuSign, delete the API
token in Documenso, or delete the client app in Ironclad.

## Troubleshooting

- **"DocuSign isn't set up on this instance yet. Add its credentials
  below."**: the integration key or secret key isn't saved. An owner adds
  them on the DocuSign card.
- **"Couldn't connect to DocuSign. Check the integration key, secret key,
  environment and redirect URI, then try again."**: most often the redirect
  URI in DocuSign doesn't match exactly (`http` vs `https`, host, port), or
  **Environment** doesn't match the account you signed in to (a demo key
  only works with **Demo**). Also try again if you spent more than 10
  minutes on DocuSign's screens.
- **"DocuSign access wasn't granted. Click Connect and choose Allow
  access."**: you clicked Cancel on DocuSign's consent screen.
- **"DocuSign access expired. Reconnect DocuSign."** under the card: the
  connection's refresh token stopped working (revoked, or unused for
  about 30 days). Click **Reconnect**.
- **"DocuSign answered 401"** or **403** after switching to production: the
  app hasn't passed go-live yet, or the production secret key or redirect
  URI is missing on the production account.
- **"Couldn't connect: Documenso answered 401."**: the API key is wrong,
  expired or deleted. Create a new token.
- **"Couldn't connect: Documenso didn't answer with JSON. Check the URL
  points at your Documenso instance."**: the URL opens something else (a
  login page, a proxy). Use the address you open Documenso at, without a
  path.
- **"Couldn't connect: That address isn't reachable from here."**: the
  Documenso URL is on a private network. Set `ALLOW_PRIVATE_FETCH=true` on
  the server, or use its public address.
- **"Couldn't connect: Ironclad answered 401"** (or 400): the client ID or
  secret is wrong, the app isn't a Client Credentials app, or it's missing
  a scope. **403** usually means the **Read as** user doesn't exist or
  can't see records.
- **"Connect DocuSign under Settings → Agreements first."** (or Documenso,
  Ironclad) when pasting a link: that service isn't connected.
- **"DocuSign doesn't have a completed agreement at that link that this
  connection can see."**: the agreement isn't fully signed yet, belongs to
  another account, or the connected user can't see it.
- An agreement is missing from the inbox: check it's completed in the
  service and visible to the connected user (DocuSign, Ironclad) or the
  token's team (Documenso), then click **Sync now**. Check **Dismissed**
  too.
- **Download signed copy** is missing: the PDF was over 25 MB, or
  downloading it failed; the server log says why. The next sync that sees
  the agreement change tries again.
