---
title: QuickBooks
description: Push invoices to QuickBooks Online and sync their status back
---

Send Consultainer invoices to QuickBooks Online so your books match, and pull
back whether they've been sent or paid there.

Setting it up has two parts: an **owner** registers an Intuit developer app
for the instance (once), then an **owner or admin** connects the
organization's QuickBooks company.

## Sandbox or production?

Intuit gives every app two sets of keys:

- **Development (sandbox)** keys only work with Intuit's test companies. Use
  them to try the integration without touching real books.
- **Production** keys work with real QuickBooks companies. Intuit only
  issues them after you complete the app's production questionnaire, and
  production redirect URIs must use `https://` on a real domain (no
  `localhost`, no IP addresses).

Whichever keys you use, set Consultainer's **Environment** to match. Sandbox
keys with **Production** selected (or the reverse) fail when you push.

## Set up the Intuit app (owner, once)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the redirect URI from Consultainer">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations** and find the **QuickBooks** card. Its
    credentials form shows a **redirect URI** with a **Copy** button, like
    `https://your-instance/api/integrations/quickbooks/callback`. Copy it.

    The URI is built from the address you're browsing on, so open
    Consultainer at the address everyone uses before copying.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an app on developer.intuit.com">
    Sign in at [developer.intuit.com](https://developer.intuit.com) (any
    Intuit account works) and open your **Dashboard**. Create an app for
    **QuickBooks Online and Payments**, give it a name, and choose the
    **Accounting** scope (`com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting`). That's the
    only scope Consultainer uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the redirect URI">
    In the app, open **Keys and credentials** (or **Settings → Redirect
    URIs**) for the environment you're setting up — development or
    production — and add the redirect URI from step 1. Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the keys">
    On the same page, copy the **Client ID** and **Client secret** for that
    environment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste them into Consultainer">
    In the QuickBooks credentials form:

    | Intuit | Consultainer field | Or in `.env` |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Client ID | **Client ID** | `QUICKBOOKS_CLIENT_ID` |
    | Client secret | **Client secret** | `QUICKBOOKS_CLIENT_SECRET` |
    | Development or production keys | **Environment**: *Sandbox* or *Production* | `QUICKBOOKS_ENVIRONMENT=sandbox` or `production` |

    Click **Save credentials**. If no environment is set, Consultainer uses
    sandbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connect your QuickBooks company (owner or admin)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    On the QuickBooks card, click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to Intuit and pick the company">
    Sign in with the Intuit account that has access to the QuickBooks
    company, choose the company (with sandbox keys, pick your sandbox
    company), and click **Connect**. You're sent back to **Settings →
    Integrations**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it worked:** the card says **Connected · company *ID***, and invoice
pages show a **QuickBooks** card with **Push to QuickBooks**.

To switch between sandbox and production, change the keys and
**Environment**, then disconnect and connect again.

## Push an invoice

On an invoice's page, owners and admins see a **QuickBooks** card. Click
**Push to QuickBooks**. Consultainer:

1. Finds the QuickBooks customer whose display name matches the client's
   name, or creates one (with the client's email). It remembers the match for
   next time.
2. Uses a service item called **Consulting Services**, creating it (on your
   first income account) if it doesn't exist.
3. Creates the invoice with the same invoice number, due date, and line items
   (description, quantity, rate).

Nothing is pushed automatically — each invoice is pushed by hand. Pushing
and syncing are for owners and admins only; members don't see the card.

Once pushed, the card shows **View in QuickBooks**. It opens the sandbox
QuickBooks site unless **Environment** (or `QUICKBOOKS_ENVIRONMENT`) is set
to production, so it matches the company you connected.

### Push again after a change

Click **Re-push to QuickBooks**. The invoice already in QuickBooks is updated
in place (customer, number, due date, and its lines replaced with the current
ones) rather than duplicated. If it was deleted in QuickBooks, a new one is
created and the card links to that one instead.

### Invoices with tax

Line items go to QuickBooks without tax, so an invoice with tax would arrive
showing a smaller total than the client was billed. Consultainer refuses to
push those: you'll see *This invoice includes tax, which can't be pushed to
QuickBooks yet — it would arrive without the tax and show a smaller total.
Enter it in QuickBooks directly instead.* Enter that invoice in QuickBooks by
hand.

## Sync status back

QuickBooks doesn't notify Consultainer when an invoice changes there, so this
is manual. On a pushed invoice, **Sync status from QuickBooks** checks it:

- Fully paid in QuickBooks (balance 0) → marked **Paid**.
- Emailed from QuickBooks while still a draft here → marked **Sent**.

Status only moves forward; a sync never downgrades an invoice.

## Disconnecting

**Disconnect** on the QuickBooks card (owner or admin) stops future pushes and
syncs. Invoices already pushed keep their link to QuickBooks.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Only owners and admins can connect integrations."** — A member clicked
  Connect. Ask an owner or admin.
- **"QuickBooks isn't set up on this instance yet — add its credentials
  below."** — The Client ID or Client secret is missing.
- **"Couldn't connect to QuickBooks. Double-check your QuickBooks app
  credentials and try again."** — Most often the redirect URI isn't
  registered for the environment whose keys you used, or doesn't match
  exactly (`http` vs `https`, host, port). Also check the Client secret, and
  try again if you spent more than 10 minutes on Intuit's screens.
- **"QuickBooks API request to … failed (401)"** or **(403)** when pushing —
  the **Environment** doesn't match the keys (sandbox keys need *Sandbox*),
  or access was revoked. Fix the environment, then disconnect and reconnect.
- **"This invoice has no line items to push."** — Add line items first.
- **"This invoice includes tax, which can't be pushed to QuickBooks yet …"**
  — See [Invoices with tax](#invoices-with-tax).
- **"Couldn't find an income account in QuickBooks to attach the default
  service item to."** — Create an income account in QuickBooks (or a
  *Consulting Services* service item yourself) and push again.
- **"This invoice hasn't been pushed to QuickBooks yet."** — Push it before
  syncing status.
- **"This organization isn't connected to QuickBooks yet."** — Connect it in
  **Settings → Integrations**.
