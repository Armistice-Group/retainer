---
title: QuickBooks
description: Push invoices to QuickBooks and sync their status back
---

Connect QuickBooks from **Settings → Integrations**. An owner or admin
authorizes access via OAuth; Consultainer requests the
`com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting` scope.

## Pushing an invoice

From an invoice's detail page, **Push to QuickBooks** creates the invoice in
QuickBooks from Consultainer's line items.

## Syncing status back

QuickBooks doesn't notify Consultainer when an invoice's status changes
there, so status sync is manual: **Sync status from QuickBooks** on the
invoice page checks the invoice's balance and email status in QuickBooks and
advances Consultainer's status accordingly (Draft → Sent → Paid). Status
only ever moves forward — a sync never downgrades an invoice that's already
further along in Consultainer than what QuickBooks reports.

## Disconnecting

Disconnecting QuickBooks from Settings → Integrations stops future pushes
and syncs; invoices already pushed aren't affected.
