Everything about getting paid lives under **Settings → Payments**: the
payment instructions printed on invoices, online payment through Stripe or
Mercury, and overdue reminders. Owners and admins can change it; members can
look.

## How clients pay

When you send an invoice (**Email to client**, or **Copy client link** —
see [Invoicing](/concepts/invoicing#how-to-send-an-invoice)), the
client opens its invoice page. There they can:

- view and **Download PDF**,
- read **How to pay** — your [payment methods](#payment-methods), and
- click **Pay now**, if online payment is set up. The button only shows on
  invoices with status **Sent**, and only once Stripe Connect has finished
  onboarding or a Mercury deposit account is chosen.

The same **Pay now** button appears next to open invoices on client and
project share pages. If both Stripe and Mercury are connected, Mercury is
used.

**Pay now** always charges the invoice's balance due: if part of it has been
paid or [credited](/concepts/deposits-and-credit) already, the client pays
only the rest. A payment is recorded on the invoice (listed in its
**Payments** card, source *Stripe* or *Mercury*) only once it has actually
gone through; when that clears the balance the invoice is marked **Paid** and
you get an **invoice paid** alert. With a Stripe card
payment that happens within seconds (through the webhook); a Stripe bank
(ACH) payment takes a few business days to clear (see
[below](#how-a-stripe-payment-works)); with Mercury, within the hour after
Mercury reports it paid.

When the client comes back from paying, the page they paid from (the invoice
page or the share page) shows a confirmation: *Thanks — payment received.*,
or, for a bank payment, *Thanks — your bank payment is processing.* If
online payment can't start, they're sent back to the same page with an
explanation instead of an error screen: *This invoice isn't open for
payment.*, *A payment for this invoice is already processing.*, or
otherwise *Online payment isn't available for this invoice right now* with a
pointer to your other payment methods (or to contact you).

## Payment methods

Payment methods are the instructions printed under **How to pay**: bank
details, a mailing address for checks, a payment link, and so on.

### Add an organization-wide method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Payments">
    The **Payment methods** card is at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add payment method and pick a type">
    **ACH bank transfer**, **Wire transfer**, **Check**, **Stripe payment
    link**, **Paddle checkout**, **PayPal**, **Venmo**, **Zelle**,
    **Crypto**, or **Other**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Required fields are marked. **Nickname** is optional (e.g. *Operating
    account*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose whether it goes on the PDF">
    **Show on invoice PDFs** puts it on the PDF as well as the online invoice
    page. Bank transfers (ACH, wire) default to off, because a PDF can be
    forwarded anywhere; the online page is only reachable through the
    invoice's private link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add" />
</Steps>

Use the arrows to reorder methods, the pencil to edit, and the bin to remove.

### Give a client its own methods

On a client's page, the **Payment methods** card works the same way. A
client's own methods are listed first, followed by the organization's. Below
them:

- Untick **Also offer the organization's payment methods** to show only the
  client's own.
- Or leave it on and untick individual organization methods this client
  shouldn't see.

When adding a method from a client page, tick **Add for the whole
organization** to save it under **Settings → Payments** instead.

Any change to payment methods raises a **billing details changed**
[alert](/concepts/alerts-and-audit), so a tampered bank account doesn't go
unnoticed.

## Collect card and ACH payments with Stripe

Consultainer uses **Stripe Connect**: the instance has one Stripe account
(the *platform*), and each organization connects its own Stripe Express
account to receive the money. On a single-company instance, the platform is
usually your own Stripe account.

### Add the Stripe keys (owner, once)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Connect in Stripe">
    In the [Stripe Dashboard](https://dashboard.stripe.com), open
    **Connect** and complete the setup Stripe asks for (it calls this your
    *platform profile*). Organizations will onboard as **Express** accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the secret key">
    In Stripe, open **Developers → API keys** and reveal the **Secret key**
    (`sk_live_…`, or `sk_test_…` in test mode).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the webhook URL from Consultainer">
    In **Settings → Payments**, the **Stripe** card shows the **webhook
    endpoint** with a **Copy** button:
    `https://your-instance/api/webhooks/stripe`. It must be reachable from
    the internet over HTTPS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the webhook in Stripe">
    In Stripe, open **Developers → Webhooks** and add an endpoint (Stripe may
    call it an *event destination*):

    - Listen to events on **Your account** (not connected accounts).
    - Endpoint URL: paste the URL from the previous step.
    - Events: select these three:
      - **`checkout.session.completed`**
      - **`checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`**
      - **`checkout.session.async_payment_failed`**

    The two `async_payment` events are how Stripe reports that a bank (ACH)
    payment cleared or bounced. Without them, ACH-paid invoices never get
    marked **Paid**.

    Save it, then reveal and copy its **Signing secret** (`whsec_…`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste both into Consultainer">
    | Stripe | Consultainer field | Or in `.env` |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Secret key | **Secret key** | `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` |
    | Webhook signing secret | **Webhook signing secret** | `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` |

    Click **Save credentials**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Test mode and live mode have separate keys and separate webhooks. Use the
secret key and signing secret from the same mode.

### Connect the organization's Stripe account (owner or admin)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Connect Stripe">
    Once the keys are saved, **Settings → Payments** shows **Collect payments
    (Stripe Connect)**. Click **Connect Stripe**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish Stripe's onboarding">
    Stripe asks for your business details and the bank account payouts go
    to, then sends you back to **Settings → Payments**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it worked:** the card shows a **Connected** badge and an **Open
Stripe dashboard** button (payouts, tax forms, bank details). If it says
*Almost there* or *Setup was started but isn't finished*, click **Continue
setup** to give Stripe what's missing.

### How a Stripe payment works

**Pay now** opens a Stripe Checkout page for the invoice's balance due in the
invoice's currency (named *Invoice INV-0042 (balance due)* once part of it is
paid). The payment methods offered are the ones turned on in
your Stripe Dashboard's payment method settings. The charge is made on the
platform account and the full amount is transferred to the organization's
connected account, so Stripe's processing fees are billed to the platform
account.

What happens next depends on how the client pays:

- **Card** — Stripe confirms the payment at checkout
  (`checkout.session.completed`), and a payment for the amount Stripe
  collected is recorded right away, which marks the invoice **Paid**.
- **Bank debit (ACH)** — the payment takes a few business days to clear. In
  the meantime the invoice stays **Sent**: its client page says *A bank
  payment for this invoice is processing*, share pages show **Bank payment
  processing** instead of **Pay now**, and the invoice's **Activity** card
  shows *Client paid by bank transfer — waiting for it to clear*. **Pay
  now** is hidden so the client can't pay twice.
  - When Stripe reports the payment cleared
    (`checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`), the payment is recorded
    and the invoice is marked **Paid**.
  - If it fails (`checkout.session.async_payment_failed`), the invoice stays
    (or goes back to) **Sent** (any payment recorded for it is removed),
    **Pay now** comes back so the client can try again, the **Activity** card
    shows *Client's bank payment failed*, and you get the **An online payment
    fails** alert saying the bank payment failed.

Each payment is recorded once, keyed on Stripe's payment intent, however many
times Stripe delivers the event. If someone recorded a payment by hand while
the client was at checkout, the client may pay more than is now due; the
extra is kept as [client credit](/concepts/deposits-and-credit) rather than
lost.

## Collect payments with Mercury

For organizations that bank with [Mercury](https://mercury.com). Clients pay
on a Mercury-hosted page by card or ACH, straight into your Mercury account.
There's no instance-wide setup; an owner or admin connects it per
organization.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an API token in Mercury">
    In Mercury, open **Settings → Tokens** and create an API token with
    **Read and Write** access. Mercury asks which IP addresses may use a
    read-write token: enter the public IP address your Consultainer server
    connects to the internet from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into Consultainer">
    In **Settings → Payments → Collect payments (Mercury)**, paste the token
    into **Mercury API token** and click **Connect Mercury**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the deposit account">
    Pick the account under **Deposit invoice payments into** and click
    **Save**. (The first active account is preselected.)
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it worked:** the card shows a **Connected** badge and your accounts
in the dropdown.

The first time a client clicks **Pay now**, Consultainer creates a Mercury
invoice (one line, *Invoice INV-0001*, for the balance due) and sends them to
its pay page; later clicks reuse it while the balance is unchanged. If the
balance changed since (you recorded a part payment, say), a new Mercury
invoice for the new balance is created instead; cancel the old one in
Mercury. Mercury doesn't email the client itself. Every hour, the scheduler
checks open Mercury invoices and, for the ones Mercury reports as paid,
records a payment for the amount that Mercury invoice asked for. The client
must have an email address on file.

## Overdue reminders

Under **Settings → Payments → Overdue reminders**, enter the numbers of days
overdue at which clients get a reminder email, e.g. `3, 7, 14` (up to ten,
1–365), and click **Save**. Leave it empty to turn reminders off.

Reminders go out from the scheduler's daily job to the client's invoice
recipients, with the invoice link. Each threshold is sent once, and turning
reminders on doesn't send a backlog. To skip a client, untick **Email overdue
reminders** on its edit page. Reminders need [email](/integrations/email).

## Troubleshooting

- **"Not set up on this instance yet. An owner can add the credentials
  here."** — Only owners can enter Stripe keys.
- **"Stripe isn't configured on this instance. Add your keys in Settings →
  Payments."** — The secret key is missing.
- Stripe onboarding errors out right away — Connect isn't turned on for the
  platform account, or the platform profile isn't finished. Finish it under
  **Connect** in the Stripe Dashboard.
- Paid in Stripe but still **Sent** here — check the webhook in Stripe
  (**Developers → Webhooks**) for failed deliveries. *Invalid signature*
  means the signing secret in Consultainer is from a different endpoint or
  mode; *Webhook not configured* means it's missing.
- An ACH-paid invoice stays on **Bank payment processing** for more than a
  week — check that the Stripe webhook also sends
  **`checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`** and
  **`checkout.session.async_payment_failed`**. If it was added late, find
  the payment in Stripe and click **Record payment** (or **Mark as paid**)
  on the invoice yourself (owners and admins).
- A client sees *Online payment isn't available for this invoice right now*
  — usually Stripe onboarding isn't complete (click **Continue setup**) or,
  with Mercury, the client has no email address on file (add one). If
  neither applies, Stripe or Mercury refused to create the checkout; your
  server log shows *[pay] Couldn't start checkout* with the reason.
- **"Couldn't connect to Mercury with that token. Check it has read-write
  access and try again."** — The token is read-only, mistyped, or the IP
  address isn't allowed in Mercury.
- **"No active Mercury checking accounts found on this token."** — The
  token's Mercury account has no active Mercury checking or savings account.
- **"Couldn't load Mercury accounts with the saved token — it may have been
  revoked."** — Disconnect and connect again with a new token.
- **"Use up to 10 whole numbers of days, 1–365."** — Fix the reminder days.
