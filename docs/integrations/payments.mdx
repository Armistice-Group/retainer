## Payment methods

Under **Settings → Payments → Payment methods**, list how clients can pay:
ACH, wire, check, a Stripe or PayPal link, Venmo, Zelle, crypto, or anything
else. They appear on the client's invoice page and share links, and — if you
choose **Show on invoice PDFs** — on the PDF. Bank details default to the
online pages only, since a PDF can be forwarded anywhere.

A client can have its own methods too (on the client page). By default a
client is offered its own methods first, then the organization's; untick
**Also offer the organization's payment methods**, or individual ones, to
change that. Adding a method from a client page can save it for the whole
organization instead.

Changes to payment methods raise a **billing details changed**
[alert](/concepts/alerts-and-audit).

## Collecting payments online

Connect one of these and sent invoices get a **Pay now** button on the
client's invoice page and share links; the invoice is marked paid when the
payment lands.

- **Stripe Connect** — **Settings → Payments → Connect Stripe** walks you
  through Stripe's onboarding; clients pay by card or ACH into your Stripe
  account. The instance needs Stripe credentials (secret key and webhook
  signing secret, entered on the same page — it shows the webhook URL to
  register).
- **Mercury** — for organizations that bank with Mercury: paste a read-write
  API token (app.mercury.com → Settings → Tokens) and choose the deposit
  account. Clients pay from a Mercury-hosted page; payment status is checked
  hourly.

## Getting invoices to clients

**Email to client** on an invoice sends it with a link to view, download and
pay it, and you're told when they open it. Overdue reminders can go out on a
schedule. See [Invoicing](/concepts/invoicing#sending-invoices-and-tracking-opens).
