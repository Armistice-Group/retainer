---
title: Documents & files
description: Link documents from Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, SharePoint and Notion, upload files, and file copies automatically
---

Documents stay where your team already keeps them. Consultainer links to them
from the client or project they belong to and controls who sees each link,
inside your team and on the client's share pages. You can also upload files to
keep them in Consultainer itself. See
[Clients & projects → Documents](/concepts/clients-and-projects#documents)
for how document visibility works.

You don't need to set anything up to link documents: pasting a link works for
anything. Connecting a service adds three things:

- **Browse and search** it inside the **Add a document** dialog instead of
  pasting links.
- **Real titles and dates.** Pasted links to that service get the item's real
  title, type and last-edited date.
- **Filing.** Owners and admins can have Consultainer keep copies of invoices
  and uploaded documents in Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive.

Setup has two parts:

1. **An owner registers an app** with each service once, for the whole
   instance, and pastes its credentials into **Settings → Integrations → Files
   & docs** (or sets them in `.env`). The guides below cover each service.
2. **Each person connects their own account** under **Profile → Files &
   docs**. Each connection uses that person's access only; nobody can browse a
   service through someone else's connection.

## Before you start

- **Who:** only an **owner** can enter or change the app credentials. Owners
  and admins can set up filing. Anyone in the organization can connect their
  own account and add documents.
- **The redirect URI.** Each service needs a redirect URI, the address the
  service sends people back to after they approve access. It is
  `https://<your Consultainer address>/api/integrations/files/<service>/callback`,
  where `<service>` is `google-drive`, `dropbox`, `onedrive` or `notion`. The
  card under **Settings → Integrations → Files & docs** shows the exact value
  for the address you're on. Open **How to set this up** under the service and
  click **Copy**.
- **One address per redirect URI.** Consultainer builds the redirect URI from
  the address in your browser. If people reach Consultainer at more than one
  address (for example `http://localhost:3113` and `https://consultainer.example.com`),
  register a redirect URI for each one. Most services only accept `https://`
  addresses, plus `http://localhost` for testing.
- **Where credentials live.** Values you set in `.env` override anything saved
  in the app. If all of a service's values come from `.env`, the card says
  **Credentials set in the server environment** and can't be edited there.
  Credentials saved in the app are encrypted with `INTEGRATION_ENCRYPTION_KEY`.

| Service | `.env` variables | Fields in Consultainer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Drive | `GOOGLE_DRIVE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_DRIVE_CLIENT_SECRET` | **Client ID**, **Client secret** |
| Dropbox | `DROPBOX_APP_KEY`, `DROPBOX_APP_SECRET` | **App key**, **App secret** |
| OneDrive & SharePoint | `MICROSOFT_CLIENT_ID`, `MICROSOFT_CLIENT_SECRET`, `MICROSOFT_TENANT_ID` (optional) | **Application (client) ID**, **Client secret**, **Directory (tenant) ID** |
| Notion | `NOTION_CLIENT_ID`, `NOTION_CLIENT_SECRET` | **OAuth client ID**, **OAuth client secret** |

## Set up Google Drive

Consultainer asks Google for these scopes: `openid`, `email`, `profile`,
`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly` (browse and look up files,
including shared drives) and `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file`
(create and update the files it files itself).

<Warning>
`drive.readonly` is a **restricted** scope. A public ("In production") app
that requests it has to pass Google's verification and a security
assessment. Avoid that with one of these:

- **Internal** audience: everyone signs in with an account in your Google
  Workspace. Connections don't expire. This is the best option if you have
  Workspace.
- **External** audience left in **Testing**, with each person added as a test
  user (up to 100). Google ends Testing-mode connections after **7 days**,
  after which people reconnect on their profile.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Google Cloud project">
    Go to [console.cloud.google.com](https://console.cloud.google.com/). In the
    project picker at the top, click **New project**, name it (for example
    *Consultainer*) and click **Create**. Make sure the new project is
    selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the Google Drive API">
    Open **APIs & Services → Library**, search for **Google Drive API**, open
    it and click **Enable**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the consent screen">
    Open **Google Auth Platform** (in older consoles, **APIs & Services → OAuth
    consent screen**) and click **Get started**. Enter an app name (people see
    it when they connect) and a support email. For **Audience**, choose
    **Internal** if everyone uses your Google Workspace, otherwise
    **External**. Add a contact email, accept the policy and click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add test users (External only)">
    Under **Audience**, leave the publishing status as **Testing**. Under
    **Test users**, click **Add users** and enter the Google address of every
    person who will connect Google Drive. Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the scopes">
    Under **Data Access**, click **Add or remove scopes**. Add
    `.../auth/drive.readonly` and `.../auth/drive.file` (paste them under
    **Manually add scopes** if they aren't in the list), click **Update**, then
    **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the OAuth client">
    Under **Clients**, click **Create client**. For **Application type**,
    choose **Web application** and give it a name. Under **Authorized redirect
    URIs**, click **Add URI** and paste the redirect URI from Consultainer
    (`.../api/integrations/files/google-drive/callback`). Click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the credentials into Consultainer">
    Copy the **Client ID** and **Client secret** now (Google may not show the
    secret again). In Consultainer, go to **Settings → Integrations → Files &
    docs → Google Drive**, paste them into **Client ID** and **Client secret**,
    and click **Save credentials**. Or set `GOOGLE_DRIVE_CLIENT_ID` and
    `GOOGLE_DRIVE_CLIENT_SECRET` in `.env` and restart.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it worked:** a green check appears next to **Google Drive** on the
card. Then [connect your account](#connect-your-account).

**Troubleshooting**

- **`Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch`** (on Google's page): the redirect URI
  in the OAuth client doesn't match the address you're on exactly. Copy it
  again from the card and add it under **Authorized redirect URIs**. Changes
  can take a few minutes to apply.
- **`Error 403: access_denied`, or "has not completed the Google verification
  process"**: the app is External and in Testing, and this person isn't a test
  user. Add them under **Audience → Test users**.
- **The picker says "The service refused: … An owner should check that
  service's setup …" right after connecting** (the message mentions the API
  not being used or being disabled): the Google Drive API isn't enabled in
  the same project as the OAuth client. Enable it, then try again.
- **The picker says "Token has been expired or revoked." after about a week:**
  the app is External and in Testing. Disconnect and reconnect on your
  profile, or switch to an Internal audience.
- **`invalid_client`** (in the server log, while the profile shows *Couldn't
  connect*): the client secret is wrong. Paste it again, or create a new one.

## Set up Dropbox

Consultainer asks Dropbox for these permissions: `account_info.read`,
`files.metadata.read`, `files.content.read`, `files.content.write`,
`sharing.read` and `sharing.write`. The sharing permissions let it make a
shared link when you pick a file; the write permission is for filing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the app">
    Go to the [Dropbox App Console](https://www.dropbox.com/developers/apps)
    and click **Create app**. Choose **Scoped access**, then **Full Dropbox**
    (not *App folder*), name the app and click **Create app**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the permissions">
    Open the app's **Permissions** tab. Tick `account_info.read`,
    `files.metadata.read`, `files.content.read`, `files.content.write`,
    `sharing.read` and `sharing.write`, then click **Submit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the redirect URI">
    Open the **Settings** tab. Under **OAuth 2 → Redirect URIs**, paste the
    redirect URI from Consultainer (`.../api/integrations/files/dropbox/callback`)
    and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the credentials into Consultainer">
    On the same **Settings** tab, copy the **App key** and the **App secret**
    (click **Show**). In Consultainer, go to **Settings → Integrations → Files &
    docs → Dropbox**, paste them into **App key** and **App secret**, and click
    **Save credentials**. Or set `DROPBOX_APP_KEY` and `DROPBOX_APP_SECRET` in
    `.env`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Set the permissions **before** anyone connects. A connection keeps the
permissions the app had when it was made, so if you change them later, each
person has to **Disconnect** and **Connect** Dropbox again on their profile.
</Warning>

A new Dropbox app is in development status, which Dropbox allows for a limited
number of users (see the app's **Settings** tab). That is usually plenty for
one team; apply for production there if you need more.

**Check it worked:** a green check appears next to **Dropbox** on the card.
Then [connect your account](#connect-your-account).

**Troubleshooting**

- **"Invalid redirect_uri"** (on Dropbox's page): add the exact redirect URI
  from the card under **OAuth 2 → Redirect URIs**.
- **Dropbox refuses to connect over a scope (`invalid_scope`), or the picker
  says "The service refused: … missing_scope …" right after connecting:** a
  permission isn't ticked on the app. Tick all six on the
  **Permissions** tab, click **Submit**, then disconnect and reconnect Dropbox
  on your profile.
- **"Dropbox wouldn't make a shared link for that item."** Your Dropbox team's
  sharing policy may block shared links, or the permissions are missing
  `sharing.write`. Paste a shared link you made in Dropbox instead.

## Set up OneDrive & SharePoint

This one app covers OneDrive (personal and work or school accounts) and
SharePoint. Consultainer asks Microsoft for these delegated permissions:
`openid`, `email`, `profile`, `offline_access`, `User.Read`,
`Files.ReadWrite.All` and `Sites.Read.All`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register the app">
    Go to the [Microsoft Entra admin center](https://entra.microsoft.com/) →
    **App registrations** (under **Identity → Applications** or **Entra ID**)
    and click **New registration**. Name it (for example *Consultainer*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can sign in">
    Under **Supported account types**, choose one:

    - **Accounts in this organizational directory only (single tenant)** to
      allow only your organization. You must then also enter the tenant ID in
      Consultainer (step 6).
    - The multitenant option that **includes personal Microsoft accounts** to
      allow anyone, including personal OneDrive. Leave the tenant ID empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the redirect URI">
    Under **Redirect URI**, choose the **Web** platform and paste the redirect
    URI from Consultainer (`.../api/integrations/files/onedrive/callback`).
    Click **Register**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a client secret">
    Open **Certificates & secrets → Client secrets** and click **New client
    secret**. Pick an expiry and click **Add**. Copy the secret's **Value**
    right away (not the **Secret ID**); it's only shown once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the API permissions">
    Open **API permissions → Add a permission → Microsoft Graph → Delegated
    permissions**. Select `offline_access`, `User.Read`, `Files.ReadWrite.All`
    and `Sites.Read.All` (plus `openid`, `email` and `profile` if they aren't
    there already). Click **Add permissions**. If your organization doesn't let
    users approve apps themselves, click **Grant admin consent for
    &lt;your organization&gt;** (this needs an Entra admin).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the credentials into Consultainer">
    From the app's **Overview**, copy the **Application (client) ID** and, for
    a single-tenant app, the **Directory (tenant) ID**. In Consultainer, go to
    **Settings → Integrations → Files & docs → OneDrive & SharePoint** and fill
    in **Application (client) ID**, **Client secret** (the Value from step 4)
    and, if single tenant, **Directory (tenant) ID**. Click **Save
    credentials**. Or set `MICROSOFT_CLIENT_ID`, `MICROSOFT_CLIENT_SECRET` and
    `MICROSOFT_TENANT_ID` in `.env`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Client secrets expire (the longest option is 24 months). When the secret
expires, nobody can connect and existing connections stop refreshing. Note
the date and, before then, create a new secret and save it in Consultainer
(**Edit credentials**) or `.env`.
</Warning>

**Check it worked:** a green check appears next to **OneDrive & SharePoint**
on the card. Then [connect your account](#connect-your-account).

**Troubleshooting**

- **`AADSTS50011`** (redirect URI mismatch): add the exact redirect URI from
  the card under the app's **Authentication → Web → Redirect URIs**.
- **`AADSTS50194`** ("not configured as a multi-tenant application"): the app
  is single tenant but Consultainer has no tenant ID. Enter the **Directory
  (tenant) ID** in Consultainer, or change the app's supported account types.
- **`AADSTS7000215`** (invalid client secret): you pasted the **Secret ID**
  instead of the **Value**, or a typo. Create a new secret and paste its
  Value.
- **`AADSTS7000222`** (expired client secret): create a new secret and save
  it.
- **"Need admin approval"** or **`AADSTS65001`**: an Entra admin has to click
  **Grant admin consent** under **API permissions**.
- **Personal accounts can't sign in:** the app's supported account types
  don't include personal Microsoft accounts, or a tenant ID is set.

## Set up Notion

Notion connects through a **public** integration. During connect, each person
picks which pages and databases Consultainer may see. Only those can be
browsed and looked up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the integration">
    Go to [notion.so/profile/integrations](https://www.notion.so/profile/integrations)
    and click **New integration**. Choose the **Public** type, name it, pick
    the workspace, and fill in the company details Notion asks for (website,
    privacy policy and terms URLs, email).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the redirect URI">
    Under **Redirect URIs**, paste the redirect URI from Consultainer
    (`.../api/integrations/files/notion/callback`). Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the capabilities">
    Under **Capabilities**, tick **Read content**. Under user capabilities,
    choose **Read user information including email addresses**. Save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the credentials into Consultainer">
    Copy the **OAuth client ID** and **OAuth client secret** from the
    integration's settings. In Consultainer, go to **Settings → Integrations →
    Files & docs → Notion**, paste them and click **Save credentials**. Or set
    `NOTION_CLIENT_ID` and `NOTION_CLIENT_SECRET` in `.env`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it worked:** a green check appears next to **Notion** on the card.
Then [connect your account](#connect-your-account).

**Troubleshooting**

- **"Missing or invalid redirect_uri"** (on Notion's page): add the exact
  redirect URI from the card to the integration.
- **The picker is empty, or shows "Only pages you shared when connecting
  Notion show up":** you didn't share any pages, or not the right ones.
  Disconnect Notion on your profile and connect again; Notion lets you pick
  pages again. You can also add the integration to a page from that page's
  connections menu in Notion.
- **A pasted Notion link gets no real title:** the page isn't shared with
  your connection. Share it as above, then click the document's refresh
  button.

## Connect your account

Anyone in the organization can do this, once an owner has set the service up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click your name or avatar, then **Profile**. Find the **Files & docs**
    card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    Click **Connect** next to the service. If the service isn't set up yet, the
    row says *Not set up on this instance* instead, and an owner has to follow
    the setup guide above first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve access">
    Sign in to the service if asked, and approve. For Notion, choose the pages
    Consultainer may see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it worked:** you're back on your profile with *Connected*, and the
service shows the account you connected (usually its email) with a green
check.

To disconnect, click **Disconnect**. Documents you already linked stay. If
filing runs through your connection, it stops until an owner or admin sets it
up again ([Set up filing](#set-up-filing)).

**Troubleshooting**

- **"Couldn't connect. Try again; if it keeps failing, ask an owner to check
  that service's redirect URI and client secret …"** The service accepted the
  sign-in but Consultainer couldn't finish it. Usually the client secret is wrong or
  expired, or the redirect URI differs from the address you started on. The
  server log has the details (`[files] Connect failed`). Also make sure you
  stay signed in to Consultainer in the same browser during the round trip.
- **"Not connected: the service refused."** followed by the service's own
  reason (for example that admin consent is required, or a scope or redirect
  URI is invalid): the service turned the request down. The reason usually
  says what to fix; check that service's troubleshooting list above.
- **"Not connected: you cancelled, or the service refused the request."** You
  clicked cancel or deny, or the service refused without giving a reason.
  If you didn't cancel, check that service's troubleshooting list above.
- **"That service isn't set up on this instance yet."** An owner needs to save
  its credentials first.
- **"Your connection expired or was revoked — reconnect it on your
  profile."** (in the picker): the service rejected the request (HTTP 401 or
  403). Disconnect and connect again.
- **"The service refused: … An owner should check that service's setup
  under Settings → Integrations → Files & docs …"** (in the picker): the
  service says the app is missing an API or permission (Google: Drive API not
  enabled; Dropbox: a permission not ticked). An owner fixes the app, then
  you reconnect on your profile.
- **A message from the service in the picker, such as "Token has been expired
  or revoked." or `invalid_grant`:** Consultainer couldn't renew your access.
  Disconnect and connect again. If it fails for everyone, the app's client
  secret may have expired or changed.

## Link a document

Anyone who can see a client or project can add documents to it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add a document">
    On the client's or project's page, find the **Documents** card and click
    **Add**. The **Link** tab is selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick or paste the document">
    If you've connected services, a browser appears at the top: choose the
    service, then search or scroll and click an item. Or paste a link into
    **Link**. Below the field, Consultainer shows what it recognized, such as
    *Google Docs file* or *SharePoint file*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    **Title** is optional; it's taken from the item when possible. Choose a
    **Type**, and on a client's page, a **Project** (or *Whole client*). Choose
    whether the client can see it and who on your team can.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add link" />
</Steps>

**Check it worked:** the document appears in the **Documents** list with the
service's icon and its title. Linked documents from a connected service show
*edited &lt;date&gt;*, the item's last-edited date.

What the browser shows for each service:

| Service | With an empty search | Searching |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Drive | Your 25 most recently modified files and folders, including shared drives | Names containing your text |
| Dropbox | The top level of your Dropbox | Dropbox search |
| OneDrive | Your recent files | Your own OneDrive. For SharePoint, paste the link instead. |
| Notion | The pages you shared, most recently edited first | Titles in those pages |

Good to know:

- **Recognized links:** Google Drive and Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms and
  Drawings; Dropbox shared links; OneDrive (`onedrive.live.com`, `1drv.ms`)
  and SharePoint (`*.sharepoint.com`); Notion (`notion.so`, `notion.com`,
  `*.notion.site`);
  and Box. Anything else is stored as a plain web link. A link must start with
  `https://` (or `http://`).
- **Real titles need your connection.** Consultainer looks a pasted link up
  with *your* connection to that service. Without one, or without access to
  the item, it uses a title guessed from the link. Box and plain links are
  never looked up.
- **Refresh.** The refresh button on a linked document re-reads its title and
  last-edited date with your connection. Only people who can manage the
  document (owners, whoever added it, and admins who can see it) can use it.
- **Dropbox picks make a shared link.** Picking a Dropbox item reuses its
  shared link or creates one with your Dropbox's default sharing settings.
  Check those if a file shouldn't be openable by anyone with the link.
- **Access still comes from the service.** A link opens in the service, so
  teammates and clients need access there too. Consultainer doesn't change
  the service's sharing.
- Removing a linked document only removes the link. The file isn't touched.

**Troubleshooting**

- **"Paste a full link, starting with https://."** The text isn't a URL. Copy
  the full address from the browser or the service's **Share** dialog.
- **"Connect it on your profile first."** or **"Connect Dropbox on your
  profile first."** You don't have a connection to that service.
- **"Couldn't search right now."** The service didn't respond. Try again.
- **The title is a guess (for example a file name from the URL):** connect the
  service, make sure you can open the item there, then click refresh.

## Upload a document

Use uploads for files that don't live in a shared service, such as a signed W-9.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add a document">
    On the client's or project's page, click **Add** on the **Documents** card,
    then the **Upload** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the file">
    Click the file field and choose a file. Optionally give it a **Title**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details and click Upload">
    Choose the **Type**, the **Project** if any, and who can see it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it worked:** the document appears in the list. Click it to open it in
Consultainer (PDFs and images open in the viewer). If [filing](#set-up-filing)
is on for uploaded documents, a copy also goes to the filing folder.

**Allowed files:** PDF, PNG, JPEG, WebP, Word (`.docx`), Excel (`.xlsx`),
PowerPoint (`.pptx`), plain text and CSV. The file picker only offers these
types. Older Office formats (`.doc`, `.xls`,
`.ppt`), GIF, HEIC and others are refused; convert them or link them instead.

**Size limit:** 5MB per file when uploads are stored in the database (the
default), or 25MB with S3-compatible storage. See
[Self-hosting → File storage](/self-hosting#file-storage) to set that up. The
dialog shows the current limit.

**Troubleshooting**

- **"Upload a PDF, image, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text or CSV file — or link it
  instead."** The file type isn't allowed (see above). If the browser reports
  a wrong type for an allowed file (some report a `.csv` as Excel, or give no
  type), Consultainer goes by the file's extension instead, so a file with
  the right extension is accepted.
- **"Files must be under 5MB — link larger ones from where they live
  instead."** (or 25MB): the file is too big. Link it instead, or set up S3
  storage to raise the limit to 25MB.
- **"Choose a file to upload."** The file is empty.

## Set up filing

Filing keeps copies of Consultainer's own documents in Google Drive, Dropbox or
OneDrive (not Notion):

- **Invoices:** the PDF, filed when the invoice is sent and updated when it's
  paid. A voided invoice's copy is updated by the next hourly run.
  Path: `<folder>/<Client>/Invoices/INV-0001.pdf`.
- **Uploaded documents:** `<folder>/<Client>/Documents/<file>`, or
  `<folder>/<Client>/<Project>/Documents/<file>` for a project's. Linked
  documents aren't copied.

Filing writes through the connection of the owner or admin who sets it up, so
the copies land in their Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the service on your profile">
    As an owner or admin, [connect](#connect-your-account) Google Drive,
    Dropbox or OneDrive under **Profile → Files & docs**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Filing card">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations** and find **Filing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where to file">
    Under **File to**, pick the service. Under **Folder**, keep *Consultainer*
    or type another name. The folder is created at the top level of your
    Drive (My Drive), Dropbox or OneDrive; if a folder with that name already
    exists there, it's reused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to file">
    Tick **Invoices**, **Uploaded documents**, or both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Set up filing" />
  <Step title="File what already exists">
    Click **File everything now** to copy invoices (sent, paid or voided) and
    uploaded documents from before filing was set up. Otherwise the hourly run
    picks them up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it worked:** the card says *Filing to &lt;service&gt; →
&lt;folder&gt;* with **Open folder**. After a sent invoice or an upload, the
file appears in the folder, and the invoice's page shows **Filed to
&lt;service&gt; ↗**.

**What happens automatically:** each item is filed when it happens. The
scheduler container calls `/api/cron/filing` every hour, which files anything
that failed or changed since. You can change **Invoices** and **Uploaded
documents** later and click **Save**. **Turn off** stops filing; copies
already filed stay where they are.

<Note>
**Moving the folder.** In Google Drive you can move the folder afterwards, for
example into a shared drive, and filing keeps working. In Dropbox and OneDrive,
filing writes by path: if you move or rename the folder, a new one is created
at the original place. To file somewhere else there, **Turn off** and set
filing up again with the new name.
</Note>

**Troubleshooting** (errors appear in red on the **Filing** card)

- **"Connect that service on your profile first — filing uses your
  connection."** You chose a service you haven't connected. Connect it first.
- **"Couldn't create the folder: …"** The service refused. Check that your
  connection still works (try the picker in **Add a document**). For Dropbox,
  check `files.content.write` is ticked; for Microsoft, `Files.ReadWrite.All`.
- **"The account filing was set up with has been disconnected — set filing up
  again."** The person who set it up disconnected the service. Click **Turn
  off**, then set filing up again with a connected account.
- **"Google Drive upload failed (…)", "Dropbox upload failed (…)", "OneDrive
  upload failed (…)":** the service rejected a file, for example because the
  account is out of storage or lost access. Fix the cause; the hourly run
  retries.
- **"Filing failed — will retry within the hour."** An unexpected error; see
  the server log (`[filing]`).

An error clears from the card on its own as soon as the next copy is filed
successfully. Once you've fixed the cause, click **File everything now**:
it clears the error and only shows it again if something still fails.
