## 1. Install it

Consultainer is self-hosted: Postgres, the app and a small scheduler, from one
compose file.

```bash
curl -O https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Armistice-Group/retainer/main/docker-compose.yml
curl -o .env https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Armistice-Group/retainer/main/.env.example
# fill in the Required section of .env (AUTH_SECRET, INTEGRATION_ENCRYPTION_KEY,
# CRON_SECRET, POSTGRES_PASSWORD) — and SETUP_TOKEN if it's on the internet
docker compose up -d
```

See [Self-hosting](/self-hosting) for every setting, upgrades, and putting it
behind a domain.

## 2. Create your organization

Open the app (port 3113 by default). A fresh install sends you to `/setup` to
create the organization and your owner account, then to a short checklist.
There's no public signup: invite your team from **Settings → Members**, or set
up single sign-on under **Settings → Security**.

## 3. Add a client

From **Clients**, add the client you're billing. Add contacts, and tick
**receives invoices** on the people invoice emails should go to. Set the
client's default payment terms (Net 30 unless you change it) and how they pay
you under **Payment methods**.

## 4. Create a project

From **Projects**, create a project under that client and pick a billing type:

- **Hourly** — billed from logged time.
- **Flat fee** — a fixed price for the whole engagement.
- **Milestone-based** — billed per deliverable as milestones are completed.

Add teammates with a bill rate, and optionally an hour budget (you're alerted
at 80% and 100%). If your organization has one member, you're added to new
projects automatically.

## 5. Track the work

Add [tasks](/concepts/tasks), or pull them in from [Linear](/integrations/linear).
Log time from **Time**, with the play button on any task, by
[connecting your calendar](/integrations/calendars) so meetings become time,
or from an AI agent over [MCP](/mcp-server).

## 6. Invoice

From **Invoices → New**, pick the client. Consultainer shows everything
eligible to bill — unbilled billable time, completed milestones, approved
expenses — and starts from the project's or client's payment terms. Generate a
draft, check the line items, then **Email to client**: they get a link to view
the invoice, download the PDF and pay, and you're told when they open it.
