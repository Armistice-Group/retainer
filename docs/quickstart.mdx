---
title: Quickstart
description: Your first hour — install, set up your organization, invite your team, log time and send your first invoice
---

This walks you from an empty server to a sent invoice. Each step names the
exact buttons in the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Consultainer">
    On a server with Docker:

    ```bash
    curl -O https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Armistice-Group/retainer/main/docker-compose.yml
    curl -o .env https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Armistice-Group/retainer/main/.env.example
    ```

    Open `.env` and fill in the **Required** section:

    ```bash
    openssl rand -base64 32   # paste as AUTH_SECRET
    openssl rand -base64 32   # paste as INTEGRATION_ENCRYPTION_KEY (keep a copy somewhere safe)
    openssl rand -hex 32      # paste as CRON_SECRET
    ```

    Also change `POSTGRES_PASSWORD`, and if the server is reachable from the
    internet, set `SETUP_TOKEN` (`openssl rand -hex 16`). Then:

    ```bash
    docker compose up -d
    ```

    [Self-hosting](/self-hosting) explains every setting, HTTPS with a
    domain, backups and upgrades.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    Open `http://<your-server>:3113`. A new install sends you to `/setup`.
    Enter the **Setup token** (if you set one), your **Organization name**,
    the **Instance URL** people will use to reach the app, and your name,
    email and password. Click **Create workspace**.

    You're now the organization's **Owner**, and you land on a checklist of
    next steps. It stays available at `/welcome`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your organization defaults">
    Go to **Settings → General**. In the **Organization** card, check:

    - **Invoice number prefix**, **Default currency**, **Default tax rate (%)**
      and **Default payment terms** (Net 30 unless you change it). New
      clients and invoices start from these.
    - **Default bill rate**: what you charge per hour, in your default
      currency. People start at this rate when they're put on a project
      (you can give someone their own rate on **Settings → Members**).
    - **Timesheet approval**: **Off**, **Contractors**, or **Everyone except
      admins**. When on, covered people submit each week from **Time**, and
      their time can't be invoiced until an admin approves it.
    - **Expense approval threshold**: members' expenses above this need
      approval before they can be invoiced.

    Click **Save changes**. On the right, under **Logo & branding**, upload
    your logo; it appears on invoice PDFs and share pages.

    Then go to **Settings → Payments** and, under **Payment methods**, add how
    clients can pay you (ACH, wire, a payment link…). They're shown on
    invoices. See [Payments](/integrations/payments) to take card and ACH
    payments online.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Go to **Settings → Members** and click **Invite member**. Enter their
    email, choose **Member** or **Admin**, and click **Create invite**. Copy
    the link and send it to them (it's also emailed if
    [email](/self-hosting#email) is set up). They set a name and password and
    they're in.

    In the member list, fill in each person's **Cost** (what an hour of
    their time costs you) so [Reports](/concepts/reports) can show profit.

    To make everyone use an authenticator app, set one up on your own
    **Profile**, then tick **Require two-factor authentication** under
    **Settings → Security**. If someone forgets their password, they can use
    **Forgot password?** on the login page, or you can make them a reset link
    from the **⋯** menu on **Settings → Members**.

    Prefer single sign-on? Set it up under **Settings → Security** instead;
    see [Team & security](/concepts/team-and-security) for what each role can
    do and step-by-step SSO setup for Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID
    and Okta.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your first client">
    Go to **Clients** and click **Add client**. Enter the **Company name** and,
    under billing, a **Billing email** and **Payment terms** if they differ
    from your defaults. Click **Create client**.

    On the client's page, click **Add contact** for the people you work with.
    Tick **Include on invoice emails** for whoever should receive invoices.

    Have many clients already? **Clients → Import** takes a CSV.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a project and set bill rates">
    On the client's page, click **New project** (or **Projects → New
    project**). Enter a **Project name**, pick a **Billing type**:

    - **Hourly**: billed from logged time.
    - **Flat fee**: a fixed **Flat fee amount** for the engagement.
    - **Milestone-based**: billed per deliverable as
      [milestones](/concepts/milestones) are completed.

    Optionally set **Budget (hours)** to get alerts at 80% and 100%. Click
    **Create project**.

    On the project page, in **Team & bill rates**, click **Add team member**,
    pick a person, check their **Hourly rate** (it's filled in from their
    default rate), and click **Add to project**. Do this for everyone who
    will log time here.

    <Warning>
      Billable time is invoiced at the person's rate on the project. If
      you're the only person in the organization, you're added to new
      projects automatically at your default rate. Set your default rate
      first (**Default bill rate** on Settings → General, or your own **Rate**
      on Settings → Members); with neither set you start at **$0/hr**, and
      you'd need to click the pencil next to your rate on each project
      before you invoice.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log time">
    Go to **Time** and click **Log time**. Choose the **Project**, the
    **Date** and **Hours**, add a **Description** of the work, keep
    **Billable** ticked, and click **Log time**. On the invoice, time is
    grouped into one line per project and person at their rate.

    You can also start a timer from any [task](/concepts/tasks),
    [connect your calendar](/integrations/calendars) so meetings become time
    entries, or log time from an AI agent over [MCP](/mcp-server). See
    [Time tracking](/concepts/time-tracking).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first invoice">
    Go to **Invoices** and click **New invoice**. Pick the client. You see
    everything ready to bill: unbilled billable time, completed milestones
    and approved expenses. Tick what to include, check **Issue date**,
    **Payment terms** and **Due date**, and click **Generate invoice**.

    The draft opens. Check the line items; you can edit them, or add a manual
    line, while it's a draft.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send it">
    With email set up, click **Email to client**, check the recipients, add
    an optional message, and click **Send invoice**. The client gets a link
    to view, download and pay it, the invoice is marked as sent, and you're
    alerted when they open it. (Owners and admins can email invoices.)

    Without email, click **Mark as sent**, then **Copy client link** and send
    the link yourself. Opens are still tracked.

    When the money arrives, click **Mark as paid** (or let
    [Stripe or Mercury](/integrations/payments) do it for you).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check it worked

- **Invoices** lists your invoice as **Sent**.
- The time entries you invoiced show as billed on **Time** and can no longer
  be edited.
- **Dashboard** shows your outstanding balance.
