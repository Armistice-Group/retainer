---
title: Milestones
description: Billing a project by deliverable instead of by the hour
---

Milestones are available on projects with the **Milestone-based** billing
type. Each milestone has a name, description, amount, and optional due date.

## Completing a milestone

Marking a milestone complete requires evidence of completion: a required
note describing what was delivered or how completion was verified, plus an
optional link and/or file attachment. This creates a record of what was
actually delivered, not just a checkbox.

A completed milestone can be reopened later — as long as it hasn't been
invoiced yet.

## Billing a milestone

Once completed, a milestone becomes eligible to appear on a generated
invoice as its own line item (quantity 1, rate = the milestone amount).
After it's been pulled onto an invoice, it's locked — it can no longer be
edited, reopened, or deleted.
