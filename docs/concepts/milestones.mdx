---
title: Milestones & deliverables
description: Bill a project by milestone, and track deliverables and their due dates
---

A project's **Milestones & deliverables** card holds two kinds of item:

- **Payment milestones** have a fixed amount and become billable once you mark
  them complete with evidence of what was delivered. Use them to bill a
  project per milestone instead of by the hour.
- **Deliverables** are tracked the same way (name, due date, completion with
  evidence) but are never billed: they don't appear on **New invoice**, aren't
  picked up by billing cycles, and don't count as unbilled work in reports.
  Use them for what you owe the client on any project, hourly or flat fee.

Both show on the [calendar](/concepts/calendar) on their due date, and you're
reminded when one is [overdue](/concepts/calendar#deadline-reminders).

The card is on every project for owners and admins. On a project whose
**Billing type** is **Milestone-based**, new items start as payment
milestones; on other projects they start as deliverables. Members see the card
read-only (on projects that aren't milestone-based, only once there's
something on it).

**Who can do this:** only owners and admins add, edit, complete, reopen and
delete milestones and deliverables, in the app and over an API key or the
MCP server alike.

## How to add a milestone or deliverable

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the card">
    Open the project and find **Milestones & deliverables**. Click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the type">
    Under **Type**, pick **Payment milestone (billed when complete)** or
    **Deliverable (not billed)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Enter a **Name**, an optional **Description**, the **Amount** (payment
    milestones only), and an optional **Due date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add**. It shows as **Pending**; a deliverable is also badged
    **Deliverable** and shows *Not billed* instead of an amount.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change one, click the pencil next to it. You can change its type too, for
example to stop billing a milestone the client no longer pays for separately.
Once a milestone is invoiced it can't be changed.

If you leave the amount empty on a payment milestone you'll see *Amount must be
greater than zero (or make it a deliverable, which isn't billed)*. A due date
that doesn't exist (say, February 30) gets *That date doesn't exist.*

## How to complete one

Completing records what was actually delivered, not just a tick.

1. Click **Mark complete**.
2. Under **Evidence of completion**, describe what was delivered and how it
   was verified (required), for example *Signed off by client on call 3/14,
   deployed to production*.
3. Optional: add a **Link** (deployed URL, PR, doc) and/or **Attach a file**
   (PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF; under 5 MB, or 25 MB when uploads go to
   S3-compatible storage).
4. Click **Mark complete**. It shows as **Completed**.

Click **View evidence** on a completed one to see the note, link and file.

## How to reopen one

Click the reopen (circular arrow) button on a completed milestone or
deliverable. It goes back to **Pending** and its evidence is cleared. You can
only reopen a milestone that hasn't been invoiced.

## How a payment milestone gets billed

A completed payment milestone that isn't on an invoice yet shows up on **New
invoice** for the project's client (and is picked up by the client's
[billing cycle](/concepts/invoicing#billing-cycles), if it has one). It
becomes its own line: *Project — Milestone: name*, quantity 1 at the
milestone's amount.

Once it's on an invoice it shows as **Invoiced** and is locked: it can't be
edited, reopened or deleted. If the invoice is a draft that gets deleted, or
the line is removed from the draft, the milestone goes back to **Completed**.

Deliverables never get here. Asking for one by id anyway (over the API or
MCP) is refused with *No eligible unbilled time entries, milestones, or
expenses were selected.*

## How to delete one

Click the bin icon and confirm. Invoiced milestones can't be deleted.

## Over MCP

`list_milestones` (any role; `billable` is `false` for a deliverable), and
`create_milestone`, `update_milestone`, `complete_milestone` and
`reopen_milestone` (owner and admin keys; a member's key gets *Only owners and
admins can manage milestones.*). `create_milestone` takes `billable` (default
`true`) and needs an `amount` for a billable one; `update_milestone` keeps the
current type and amount when you leave them out. Due dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`.
See the [MCP server](/mcp-server).
