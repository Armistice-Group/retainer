---
title: Milestones
description: Bill a project by deliverable instead of by the hour
---

Milestones let you bill a project per deliverable: each one has a fixed
amount, and becomes billable once you mark it complete with evidence of what
was delivered. They're available on projects whose **Billing type** is
**Milestone-based** (set it with **Edit** on the project page).

Only owners and admins add, edit, complete, reopen and delete milestones, in
the app and over an API key or the MCP server alike. Members see them
read-only.

## How to add a milestone

1. Open the project and find the **Milestones** card.
2. Click **Add milestone**.
3. Enter a **Name**, an optional **Description**, the **Amount**, and an
   optional **Due date**.
4. Click **Add milestone**. It shows as **Pending**.

To change a milestone's name, amount or due date, delete it and add it again
(or use the `update_milestone` MCP tool). Once it's invoiced it can't be
changed.

## How to complete a milestone

Completing a milestone records what was actually delivered, not just a tick.

1. Click **Mark complete** on the milestone.
2. Under **Evidence of completion**, describe what was delivered and how it
   was verified (required), for example *Signed off by client on call 3/14,
   deployed to production*.
3. Optional: add a **Link** (deployed URL, PR, doc) and/or **Attach a file**
   (PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF; under 5 MB, or 25 MB when uploads go to
   S3-compatible storage).
4. Click **Mark complete**. It shows as **Completed**.

Click **View evidence** on a completed milestone to see the note, link and
file.

## How to reopen a milestone

Click the reopen (circular arrow) button on a completed milestone. It goes
back to **Pending** and its evidence is cleared. You can only reopen a
milestone that hasn't been invoiced.

## How a milestone gets billed

A completed milestone that isn't on an invoice yet shows up on **New invoice**
for the project's client (and is picked up by the client's
[billing cycle](/concepts/invoicing#billing-cycles), if it has one). It
becomes its own line: *Project — Milestone: name*, quantity 1 at the
milestone's amount.

Once it's on an invoice it shows as **Invoiced** and is locked: it can't be
edited, reopened or deleted. If the invoice is a draft that gets deleted, or
the line is removed from the draft, the milestone goes back to **Completed**.

## How to delete a milestone

Click the bin icon on the milestone and confirm. Invoiced milestones can't be
deleted.

## Over MCP

`list_milestones` (any role), and `create_milestone`, `update_milestone`,
`complete_milestone` and `reopen_milestone` (owner and admin keys; a member's
key gets *Only owners and admins can manage milestones.*). See the
[MCP server](/mcp-server).
