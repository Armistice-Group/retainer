---
title: Time Tracking
description: Log hours with a timer or by hand, and submit weekly timesheets for approval
---

Time entries are what hourly work is invoiced from. Each entry is hours on a
project (and optionally a task) on a given day. You can log time with a timer
or by hand, from the **Time** page, a task, or an AI agent over MCP.

## How to log time by hand

1. Go to **Time** and click **Log time** (or click **Log time** on a task's
   sheet, which fills in the project and task).
2. Pick the **Project** and, optionally, the **Task**.
3. Set the **Date** and **Hours** (decimal hours, up to 24 in one entry; for
   example `1.5` for an hour and a half).
4. Optional: add a **Description**. It appears in exports and timesheet
   reviews.
5. Leave **Billable** ticked for client work. Untick it for internal or
   non-billable work: it still counts toward budgets and utilization but never
   goes on an invoice.
6. Owners and admins also see:
   - **Assigned to** — log the time for someone else. It has to be someone
     in your organization (*That person isn't a member of this
     organization.*).
   - **Rate override** — bill this entry at a different hourly rate. Leave it
     blank to use the person's rate on the project.
7. Click **Log time**. The entry appears under its day.

Each time someone logs time, the other owners and admins get the **Someone
logs time** alert: in the app always, and in Slack (on by default) or email
as chosen under [Settings → Alerts](/concepts/alerts-and-audit). It isn't
sent when the only owner or admin logs their own time.

## How to use the timer

1. Click **Start timer** in the top bar, pick the **Project** (and optionally
   a **Task**, **Description** and **Billable**), and click **Start timer**.
   Or click the play button on any task to start a timer on that task.
2. The running time shows in the top bar.
3. Click **Stop**. The elapsed time is logged as an entry dated today,
   rounded to the nearest 0.01 hour (36 seconds).

- **Only one timer runs at a time.** Starting a new one stops the running one
  and logs its time first.
- **Discard:** the **X** next to the running timer throws it away without
  logging anything.
- A timer stopped after less than about 18 seconds logs nothing.
- If the time can't be logged (for example the week is
  [locked](#timesheet-approval)), you see the error and the timer keeps
  running, so nothing is lost.

Over MCP: `start_timer`, `stop_timer`, `get_active_timer`.

## How to see your week, or the team's

**Time** shows one week at a time, Monday to Sunday, with the total logged.
Use the arrows to move between weeks and **This week** to come back.

Owners and admins also get tabs (members see the **Meetings** tab once
they've connected a calendar):

- **My time** — your own entries.
- **Team** — everyone's entries, with a person filter.
- **Approvals** — timesheets waiting for review, when
  [timesheet approval](#timesheet-approval) is on.
- **Meetings** — see [Logging meetings](#logging-meetings-from-your-calendar).

Entries are badged **Non-billable**, **Rate override**, **Approved** or
**Invoiced** where that applies.

## How to edit or delete an entry

Click the pencil on the entry to edit it, or the bin icon to delete it (you're
asked to confirm). You can change your own entries; owners and admins can
change anyone's from the **Team** tab. You can only move an entry to a
project you can see.

An entry can't be changed or deleted once it's on an invoice: it shows
**Invoiced** and has no pencil or bin. To change invoiced time, delete the
draft invoice (or remove its line) first, which makes the time unbilled again.
Once an invoice is sent, its time stays locked.

If [timesheet approval](#timesheet-approval) covers you, you also can't change
time in a week that's submitted or approved.

## How to export time

Click **Export CSV** on the **Time** page. It exports the week you're looking
at, and on the **Team** tab respects the person filter. The file opens in
Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

## Overhead

Under **Settings → General → Overhead (%)** (default 0), an owner or admin can
add a percentage to hourly rates on invoices. It's applied when an invoice is
generated and baked into the rate shown on each hourly line, not added as a
separate line. Time entries and project rates don't change.

## Timesheet approval

Timesheet approval makes people submit each week's time, and an owner or admin
approve it, before that time can be invoiced. It's off by default.

### How to turn it on

1. Go to **Settings → General** (owners and admins).
2. Set **Timesheet approval** to:
   - **Contractors** — only members whose employment type is Contractor
     (set under **Settings → Members**).
   - **Everyone except admins** — every member who isn't an owner or admin.
3. Save.

Owners and admins never need approval for their own time.

### How to submit your week

If approval covers you, the **Time** page shows a **Timesheet** bar for the
week you're viewing.

1. Log the week's time.
2. Click **Submit week**. The bar shows **Waiting for approval**, and owners
   and admins get a *timesheet submitted* alert.
3. While it's waiting, the week is locked. To change something, click
   **Recall**, edit, and submit again.
4. When it's reviewed you get a notification:
   - **Approved** — the week stays locked and its billable time can now be
     invoiced.
   - **Changes requested** — the reviewer's note shows in the bar. Fix the
     time and click **Resubmit week**.

You can't submit a week with no time logged.

### How to approve timesheets

1. Go to **Time → Approvals** (owners and admins).
2. Each submitted week shows the person, the week, total and billable hours,
   and every entry.
3. Click **Approve**, or **Request changes**, type what needs changing (they
   see it), and click **Send back**.

### What approval changes

- **New invoice** and [billing cycles](/concepts/invoicing#billing-cycles)
  only pick up approved time, plus time from people approval doesn't cover.
  **New invoice** says how many hours are still waiting for approval. A
  billing cycle leaves unapproved time for its next run.
- Owners and admins can still add or edit time in an approved week; what they
  add counts as approved.
- An approved week can be sent back (reopened) with the MCP
  `review_timesheet` tool. Time from it that's already invoiced stays
  invoiced.

Over MCP: `submit_timesheet`, `list_pending_timesheets`, `review_timesheet`.

## Logging meetings from your calendar

Connect your calendar's private iCal (.ics) link on your **Profile** and
your meetings appear under **Time → Meetings**, each with a suggested project.
Logging one adds its length as time on that project, dated the meeting's day;
you can also ignore it. Setup and details are in
[Calendars](/integrations/calendars).
