## Logging time

From **Time**, log an entry against a project and, optionally, a task. Each
entry has:

- **Date and hours** (up to 24 in a single entry)
- **Description** (optional)
- **Billable** — on by default; turn off for internal or non-billable work
- **Rate override** — an owner or admin can set a specific rate for that
  entry instead of the project member's standard rate

## My time vs. team time

Everyone sees their own week by default. Owners and admins can switch to a
team view, see everyone's logged hours, and filter by person.

## Editing and deleting entries

A time entry can be edited or deleted by the person who logged it (or an
owner/admin) — but only until it's been pulled onto an invoice. Once
invoiced, an entry is locked to keep the invoice and the underlying hours in
sync.

## Exporting

The Time page has an **Export CSV** button that respects whatever filters
are active (week, team/person view) — it opens cleanly in Excel or Google
Sheets.

## Overhead

An organization can set an overhead percentage (Settings → General, default
0%). It's applied to the billed rate on hourly invoice line items at
generation time — baked directly into the rate shown on the invoice, not
broken out as a separate line.

## Timesheet approval

Under **Settings → General → Timesheet approval**, an organization can require
people to submit their time each week before it's invoiced — for
**Contractors** only, or **Everyone except admins**. It's off by default.

- On **Time**, people it covers see the week's status and a **Submit week**
  button. A submitted week is locked; **Recall** takes it back to edit.
- Owners and admins review under **Time → Approvals**: **Approve**, or
  **Request changes** with a note the person sees.
- Only approved time (plus time from people approval doesn't cover) shows up
  when generating invoices, manually or on a billing cycle. New invoice notes
  how many hours are still waiting.
- Admins can still edit time in an approved week; sending an approved week back
  reopens it (time already invoiced stays invoiced).

Over MCP: `submit_timesheet`, `list_pending_timesheets`, `review_timesheet`.

## Logging meetings from your calendar

On your **Profile**, connect a calendar with its secret iCal (.ics) link — Google
Calendar, Outlook / Microsoft 365 and iCloud all have one (the card says where).
Every hour, meetings from the last two weeks that have ended show up under
**Time → Meetings** (you're notified when there are some to sort), each with a
suggested project:

- the project you logged earlier meetings in the same recurring series to;
- otherwise a client whose email domain matches the attendees (from its contacts,
  billing email or website), on the project you've logged most to for them;
- otherwise a project or client named in the meeting title.

**Log** adds the meeting's length as time on the project (billable unless you
untick it); **Ignore** skips it. Tick **Always for this series** to sort the rest
of a recurring meeting the same way automatically. All-day events, free/busy
blocks, cancelled meetings and ones you declined are left out. Only you see your
meetings, and the calendar link is stored encrypted.
