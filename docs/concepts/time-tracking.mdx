## Logging time

From **Time**, log an entry against a project and, optionally, a task. Each
entry has:

- **Date and hours** (up to 24 in a single entry)
- **Description** (optional)
- **Billable** — on by default; turn off for internal or non-billable work
- **Rate override** — an owner or admin can set a specific rate for that
  entry instead of the project member's standard rate

## My time vs. team time

Everyone sees their own week by default. Owners and admins can switch to a
team view, see everyone's logged hours, and filter by person.

## Editing and deleting entries

A time entry can be edited or deleted by the person who logged it (or an
owner/admin) — but only until it's been pulled onto an invoice. Once
invoiced, an entry is locked to keep the invoice and the underlying hours in
sync.

## Exporting

The Time page has an **Export CSV** button that respects whatever filters
are active (week, team/person view) — it opens cleanly in Excel or Google
Sheets.

## Overhead

An organization can set an overhead percentage (Settings → General, default
0%). It's applied to the billed rate on hourly invoice line items at
generation time — baked directly into the rate shown on the invoice, not
broken out as a separate line.
