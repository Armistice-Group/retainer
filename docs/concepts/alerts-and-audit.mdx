## Alerts

Owners and admins always see alerts in Consultainer (the bell). Under
**Settings → Alerts** they choose, per alert, whether it also goes to
**Slack** and/or **email**, and who alert emails go to (by default, the
organization's owners).

| Alert | When | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A client opens an invoice | The emailed invoice, its client link, or its PDF is opened by the client — first open, then again after a day. | Slack + email |
| Billing or payment details change | Payment methods or instructions, billing contacts and emails, payment terms, bill or cost rates, billing cycles, recurring invoices, payment connections. Says who changed what, never the payment details themselves. | Slack + email |
| Access or security settings change | Roles changed, members removed, invites, API keys created or revoked, single sign-on and sign-in rules, two-factor turned off, sign-in email changed, passkeys removed — and anyone deleting 20+ records within 10 minutes. | Slack + email |
| An invoice is sent / paid | | Slack + email |
| An invoice becomes overdue | | Slack |
| Budget | A project reaches 80% / 100% of its hour budget, or a task passes its estimate (its assignee is told too). | Slack |
| A timesheet is submitted | | In the app only |
| Weekly digest | See below. | Email |

**Slack** needs an [incoming webhook](https://api.slack.com/messaging/webhooks)
URL in **Settings → General**. **Email** needs email set up on the instance
(see [Self-hosting](/self-hosting#email)).

People also get their own notifications in the app — task assigned, comments
and @mentions on tasks they watch, timesheets reviewed, meetings to sort.

## Weekly digest

Each Monday, last week in one email: hours (in total and per person,
billable), invoiced, paid, not yet invoiced, overdue invoices, projects near or
over budget, and timesheets waiting for approval. **Preview** on Settings →
Alerts shows this week's.

## Audit log

**Settings → Audit log** (owners and admins) records every change to
organization data — clients, contacts, documents, projects, members, tasks,
comments, time, expenses, invoices, payment methods, settings and
integrations — plus sign-ins, document views and downloads, and audit log
exports. Each entry has who made it (or *System* for scheduled jobs and
webhooks), whether it came through the API or MCP, the IP address and browser,
and for updates each field's before and after. Secrets show as `[redacted]`.

Filter by person, action, record type and dates, and export to CSV. The log is
also available over the [REST API](/api-reference/audit-log) and MCP
(`list_audit_log`).
