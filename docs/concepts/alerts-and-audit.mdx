---
title: Alerts & audit log
description: What owners and admins are told about, where it goes, and the record of who changed what
---

Alerts tell owners and admins when something they should know about happens:
a client opens an invoice, someone changes payment details, a project runs
over budget. The audit log is the permanent record behind them: every change,
who made it, and from where.

**Who can do this:** owners and admins. Members don't see **Settings → Alerts**
or **Settings → Audit log**.

## Where alerts go

Every alert goes to three places, depending on your settings:

- **In the app (always).** Every owner and admin gets it under the bell in the
  top bar and on the **Notifications** page. You can't turn this off. (The
  task deadline reminders are the exception: they go to the task's assignee
  instead.) The
  person who caused the alert (for example, the admin who changed a role)
  isn't notified about their own change.
- **Slack (optional).** Posted to one channel through a Slack incoming
  webhook.
- **Email (optional).** Sent to the addresses in **Send alert emails to**, or
  to every owner if that box is empty.

You choose Slack and email separately for each alert on **Settings → Alerts**.

## The alerts

| Alert | What triggers it | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A client opens an invoice | Someone opens the invoice email, the invoice's client link, or its PDF. Drafts don't count, and neither do opens by someone signed in to your organization. You hear about the first open, then again only if it's opened more than 24 hours after the last alert. | Slack + email |
| Billing or payment details change | Someone adds, changes or removes: organization or client payment methods and payment instructions; client billing email, billing address, payment terms or which payment methods they're offered; invoice contacts; a project's billing type, flat fee or payment terms; a member's bill rate on a project, default bill rate or cost rate; billing cycles; recurring invoices; the Mercury or QuickBooks connection; or the organization's currency, default bill rate, tax rate, overhead, payment terms, invoice prefix, external billing link or Stripe account. The alert says who changed what. Payment details, payment instructions and cost rates are never included, only that they changed. | Slack + email |
| Access or security settings change | A member's role changes, a member is removed, someone is invited, an API key is created or revoked, single sign-on is set up, changed or removed, the sign-in domain or auto-join setting changes, two-factor is turned off, **Require two-factor authentication** is turned on or off, someone creates a password reset link for a member or a member resets their password, a sign-in email changes, or a passkey is removed. Also: one person deletes 20 or more records within 10 minutes (one alert per 10 minutes). | Slack + email |
| An invoice is sent | A draft invoice is marked sent or emailed to the client, from the app or the MCP server, or by a recurring invoice or billing cycle set to mark its invoices sent automatically. | Slack + email |
| An invoice is paid | An invoice is paid in full: someone records the last payment or marks it paid, credit covers the rest, a client's Stripe payment goes through (for a bank payment, when it clears), or a Mercury or QuickBooks sync finds the payment. | Slack + email |
| A part payment is received | A payment is recorded on an invoice that still has a balance due afterwards, by hand or online. | Slack |
| An online payment fails | A bank (ACH) payment a client made through **Pay now** (Stripe) bounced after it was submitted. The invoice is open for payment again. | Slack + email |
| A client accepts an estimate | A client accepts an [estimate](/concepts/estimates) on its client page, or someone marks it accepted. The person who marked it doesn't get the in-app alert. | Slack + email |
| A client declines an estimate | A client declines an estimate on its client page, or someone marks it declined. | Slack + email |
| An invoice becomes overdue | The daily job finds a sent invoice at least one day past its due date. Each invoice alerts once. | Slack |
| A project nears its budget or a task passes its estimate | Logged hours reach 80% and then 100% of a project's hour budget, or a task's logged hours go over its estimate. The task's assignee is notified in the app too. Each level alerts once, and re-arms if hours drop back or the budget grows. | Slack |
| Weekly digest (Mondays) | See [Weekly digest](#weekly-digest). Not shown in the app. | Email |
| A timesheet is submitted for approval | A member submits a week of time. | In the app only |
| An expense is waiting for approval | A member logs an [expense](/concepts/expenses#approval) above the approval threshold, from the app or the MCP server. Not sent for expenses that are approved automatically. On a [confidential project](/concepts/clients-and-projects), Slack and email say only *… logged a … expense on a confidential project that's waiting for approval.*, without the project or description; the in-app alert has them, since owners and admins can see every project. | Slack + email |
| Someone logs time | Anyone logs time. Not sent when the person logging is the only owner or admin, and never to the person who logged it. | Slack |
| A recurring invoice or billing cycle needs review | A recurring invoice or billing cycle generates a draft for you to review and send, or one set to [send automatically](/concepts/invoicing#sending-automatically) couldn't be emailed (it's still marked sent; the alert says why). Ones emailed automatically use **An invoice is sent** instead. | Slack |
| A scheduled invoice couldn't be sent | A draft [scheduled to send](/concepts/invoicing#how-to-schedule-an-invoice) couldn't be emailed when its time came: email isn't set up, the client has no address, or delivery failed. The invoice stays a draft and isn't retried until someone sends or reschedules it; the alert and the invoice say why. | Slack + email |
| A task is due tomorrow | The daily job finds a task due tomorrow that isn't done. Goes only to the task's assignee: in the app, and by email to their own address (not the alert email list). Not sent for unassigned tasks. See [Deadline reminders](/concepts/calendar#deadline-reminders). | Email |
| A task or milestone is overdue | The daily job finds a task, milestone or deliverable past its due date and not done. An overdue task goes only to its assignee (in the app and by email to them); an overdue milestone or deliverable goes to owners, admins and the people on the project in the app, and to Slack and the alert email list. On a [confidential project](/concepts/clients-and-projects), Slack and email leave out the task, milestone and project names. | Slack + email |

## How to connect Slack

You need an owner or admin account in Consultainer and permission to add apps
to your Slack workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Slack app">
    Go to [api.slack.com/apps](https://api.slack.com/apps) and click **Create
    New App**, then **From scratch**. Name it (for example, *Consultainer*),
    pick your workspace, and click **Create App**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on incoming webhooks">
    In the app's left sidebar, open **Incoming Webhooks** and switch
    **Activate Incoming Webhooks** on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a channel">
    At the bottom of the same page, click **Add New Webhook** (older
    workspaces show **Add New Webhook to Workspace**). Choose the channel
    alerts should go to and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the webhook URL">
    Back on **Incoming Webhooks**, copy the new **Webhook URL**. It looks like
    `https://hooks.slack.com/services/T000/B000/XXXX`. Treat it like a
    password: anyone with it can post to that channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into Consultainer">
    In Consultainer, go to **Settings → General**, paste the URL into **Slack
    webhook URL**, and click **Save changes**. (The webhook lives on the
    General page, not on Alerts.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which alerts go to Slack">
    Go to **Settings → Alerts**, tick the **Slack** box for each alert you
    want in Slack, and click **Save alerts**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it worked:** the "Slack isn't connected" note at the top of
**Settings → Alerts** is gone. There's no test button; to see a message,
trigger an alert you've ticked for Slack, for example by marking a test
invoice as sent.

**Troubleshooting**

- **"Enter the Slack webhook URL, e.g. https://hooks.slack.com/services/…"**
  or **"Use the https:// webhook URL Slack gives you."** when saving: the
  field only accepts a full URL starting with `https://`. Copy the whole
  webhook URL again.
- **Nothing arrives in Slack:** Consultainer only checks that the URL starts
  with `https://` when you save it, and a failed post never shows an error in
  the app. Check the app container's logs (`docker compose logs
  consultainer-app`) for `Failed to post to Slack webhook:` followed by the
  reason:
  - *The server answered 404.* (or 403) — the webhook was removed or its
    channel was archived. Create a new webhook and paste it in.
  - *That address isn't reachable from here.* — the URL points at a private
    or internal address, which is refused unless the server admin sets
    `ALLOW_PRIVATE_FETCH=true` (see [Self-hosting](/self-hosting#optional)).
  - *The server took too long.* or *Couldn't reach the server (…)* — the
    server can't reach Slack within 5 seconds; check its outbound internet
    access.
- **Only some alerts arrive:** check that alert's **Slack** box on
  **Settings → Alerts** and that you clicked **Save alerts**.

## How to set up alert emails

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make sure email works on your instance">
    Alert emails are sent with Resend. If **Settings → Alerts** says "Email
    isn't set up on this instance", follow the **Email** card on **Settings →
    Integrations** (or set `RESEND_API_KEY`; see
    [Self-hosting](/self-hosting#email)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who gets them">
    On **Settings → Alerts**, enter addresses in **Send alert emails to**,
    separated by commas, spaces or new lines (up to 20). Leave it empty to
    email the organization's owners.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which alerts are emailed">
    Tick the **Email** box for each alert you want by email, then click
    **Save alerts**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it worked:** you see **Saved.** next to the button, and the next
alert you ticked arrives by email with a link back to Consultainer.

**Troubleshooting**

- **"Enter up to 20 valid email addresses."** One of the addresses is
  malformed, or there are more than 20.
- **Nothing arrives:** check that email is set up (above) and look in spam.
  With no addresses entered, only owners are emailed, not admins.

## Your own notifications

Everyone, not just owners and admins, gets notifications in the app under the
bell and on the **Notifications** page (click **Mark all read** to clear
them). Besides the org alerts above (owners and admins only), you're notified
when:

- you're assigned a task, or added to a project;
- someone comments on a task you're assigned to or watching, or @mentions you;
- your timesheet is approved or sent back;
- your expense is approved or rejected (also emailed to you when email is set
  up);
- meetings from your connected calendar are waiting to be sorted;
- (owners and admins) someone joins the organization.

Personal notifications are in-app only, except the expense one above. They
don't go to Slack.

## Weekly digest

A summary of last week, for owners (or whoever is in **Send alert emails
to**).

**When it sends:** the scheduler's daily job sends it on Monday (UTC), or on
Tuesday if Monday's run didn't happen, once per week. The daily job runs about
a minute after the `scheduler` container starts and every 24 hours after
that, so the exact time of day depends on when you started it.

**What it covers** (Monday to Sunday of last week, UTC):

- hours logged and billable hours;
- invoiced (invoices issued last week, not counting deposits) and received
  (payments received last week, part payments included);
- not yet invoiced: all billable work not yet on an invoice;
- hours by person;
- overdue invoices;
- active projects past 80% of their budget, or over it;
- timesheets waiting for approval, if any.

**How to preview it:** on **Settings → Alerts**, click **Preview** next to
**Weekly digest (Mondays)**. It opens last week's digest email in a new tab.
The email's **Open Reports** button opens [Reports](/concepts/reports) for
that same week.

**How to turn it on or off:** tick or untick **Email** and **Slack** on the
**Weekly digest (Mondays)** row and click **Save alerts**. With both unticked
it isn't sent. The Slack version is the same summary as a message.

## Audit log

**Settings → Audit log** records every change to your organization's data,
newest first.

**What's recorded**

- Creates, updates and deletes of: the organization's settings, members and
  invites, users' accounts and passkeys, API keys, single sign-on, QuickBooks,
  Mercury and Linear connections, linked Linear projects, clients, contacts,
  client documents, links, projects and project members, milestones, tasks and
  task comments, time entries, timesheets, expenses, estimates, invoices, recurring
  invoices, billing cycles, payment methods, invoice payments, credit notes and
  credit applied to invoices.
- Sign-ins (with the sign-in method), client documents viewed and downloaded,
  and audit log exports.

Each entry shows who did it, what they did, the record (linked where it still
exists), when (in UTC), and the IP address (hover it for the browser). For
updates, click the field summary to see each field's before and after.

- **System** means no person was involved: a scheduled job, or an incoming
  webhook from Stripe or Linear.
- An **API** badge means it was made with an API key, through the
  [REST API](/api-reference/introduction) or the [MCP server](/mcp-server).
- Passwords, tokens, secrets and similar fields show as `[redacted]`, and so
  do payment-method details (account and routing numbers, IBANs and the
  like) and payment instructions. The entry still shows that they changed.
- Bookkeeping changes the app makes on its own (last-used times, view counts,
  next invoice number and similar) aren't recorded.

### How to find something

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick your filters">
    At the top of **Settings → Audit log**, choose **Who** (a person, or
    **System**), **Action**, **Record** type, and a **From** and **To** date
    (both inclusive).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Filter">
    Results show 50 at a time. Use **Older →** and **← Newer** to page. Click
    **Clear** to remove the filters.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Links in alerts (for example, from a security alert about API keys) open the
audit log already filtered.

### How to export it

Click **Export CSV**. The file uses your current filters and holds up to
50,000 entries; narrow the dates for more. It has one row per entry with
`time`, `actor`, `actor_email`, `via` (`web`, `api`, `system`, or `share` for a client contact verifying on a client link), `action`,
`summary`, `record_type`, `record_id`, `record`, `count`, `changes` (JSON),
`ip_address` and `user_agent`. Each export is itself recorded in the log.

### Retention

Entries are kept until you delete them from the database. Consultainer never
prunes the log. It starts empty: changes made before the audit log existed
aren't in it.

The log is also available over the [REST API](/api-reference/audit-log) and
the MCP server (`list_audit_log`), for owner and admin keys.
