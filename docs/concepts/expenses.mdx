---
title: Expenses
description: Log reimbursable costs with receipts, get them approved, and bill them
---

Expenses track costs you bill back to the client, such as a software
license, hardware or travel. Each expense belongs to a project and goes on an
invoice as its own line, like a milestone. Every project has an **Expenses**
card, whatever its billing type.

## How to log an expense

Anyone who can see the project can log an expense.

1. Open the project and click **Log expense** on the **Expenses** card.
2. Enter a **Description** (for example *Figma seat for this engagement*), the
   **Amount** and the **Date** you incurred it.
3. Optional: add a **Category**.
4. Optional: attach a **Receipt**: PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF, under 5 MB (25 MB
   when uploads go to S3-compatible storage).
5. Click **Log expense**.

The expense appears on the card as **Approved** or **Pending** (see below).
Click **Receipt** on an expense to open its receipt.

## Approval

Expenses above a threshold need an owner or admin to approve them before they
can be invoiced. Set the threshold under **Settings → General → Expense
approval threshold** (owners and admins; $100 by default, in your default
currency).

- An expense logged by an **owner or admin** is approved automatically,
  whatever the amount.
- An expense logged by a **member** is approved automatically if it's at or
  under the threshold.
- A member's expense **above** the threshold is **Pending** until an owner or
  admin approves or rejects it.

### How to approve or reject an expense

1. Open the project. Pending expenses show **Approve** and **Reject** buttons
   on the **Expenses** card (owners and admins only).
2. Click **Approve** to make it billable, or **Reject** to mark it
   **Rejected**. A rejected expense is never invoiced.

### Who hears about it

- **Owners and admins**, when a member's expense goes **Pending**: the
  **An expense is waiting for approval** alert, in the app and, by default,
  in Slack and by email (change it on **Settings → Alerts**; see
  [Alerts](/concepts/alerts-and-audit#the-alerts)). On a confidential
  project, Slack and email leave out the project and description. The
  dashboard also shows **Expenses waiting for approval** with the count and
  the oldest five; click one to open its project's **Expenses** card.
- **The person who logged it**, when it's approved or rejected: a
  notification in the app, and an email (*Your … expense was approved* or
  *… rejected*) when [email](/integrations/email) is set up. If they're no
  longer on a confidential project, the message leaves the project out.

This works the same for expenses logged or decided over MCP.

**Check it worked:** log an expense above the threshold as a member, then
open the dashboard as an owner or admin: the **Expenses waiting for approval**
card lists it, and the bell has *… logged a … expense on … that's waiting for
approval*.

**Troubleshooting:**

- **No alert for an expense:** it was approved automatically (logged by an
  owner or admin, or at or under the threshold).
- **No Slack message or email:** check the alert's switches on **Settings →
  Alerts**, the Slack webhook, and that [email](/integrations/email) is set
  up. Alert emails go to **Send alert emails to**, or to the owners if it's
  empty.

## How an expense gets billed

Approved expenses that aren't on an invoice yet show up on **New invoice** for
the project's client, and are picked up by the client's
[billing cycle](/concepts/invoicing#billing-cycles) if it has one. Each
becomes its own line: *Project — Expense: description*, quantity 1 at the
expense's amount.

Once it's on an invoice, an expense is locked and can't be deleted. If that
invoice is a draft and gets deleted (or the line is removed), the expense
becomes billable again.

## How to delete an expense

Click the bin icon on the expense and confirm. You can delete your own
expenses; owners and admins can delete anyone's. Invoiced expenses can't be
deleted.

## Over MCP

`list_expenses`, `log_expense`, `approve_expense` and `reject_expense`. See
