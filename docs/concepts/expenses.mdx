---
title: Expenses
description: Logging and billing reimbursable costs like software or hardware
---

Expenses let you track and bill reimbursable costs on a project — a software
license, a piece of hardware, travel — the same way time and milestones are
tracked and billed.

## Logging an expense

From a project's **Expenses** card, log a description, amount, date, an
optional category, and an optional receipt (PDF, PNG, or JPEG).

## Approval

Expense approval is threshold-based, controlled by **Expense approval
threshold** in Settings → General (defaults to $100):

- An owner or admin's own logged expense is **always auto-approved**,
  regardless of amount.
- A regular member's expense is **auto-approved** if it's at or under the
  threshold.
- A regular member's expense **above** the threshold is held **Pending**
  until an owner or admin approves or rejects it from the project page.

## Billing an expense

Only **Approved**, unbilled expenses are eligible to appear on a generated
invoice — as their own line item, the same way a milestone is. Once
invoiced, an expense is locked.

## Deleting an expense

The person who submitted an expense (or an owner/admin) can delete it, as
long as it hasn't been invoiced yet.
