---
title: Invoicing
description: Generate invoices from unbilled work, send them, track opens, and get paid
---

An invoice bills one client for work you've already recorded: billable time,
completed payment milestones and approved expenses (never
[deliverables](/concepts/milestones), which aren't billed). You generate a draft, check it,
send it, and record payments against it until it's paid (or let online
payments do that). For money taken up front, bill a
[deposit](/concepts/deposits-and-credit); to give money back as credit,
issue a [credit note](/concepts/deposits-and-credit#credit-notes).

**Who can do what:** in the app, anyone in the organization can generate
invoices and edit drafts. Sending an invoice (**Email to client** or **Mark
as sent**), recording payments and marking it paid, applying credit, voiding
it, deleting a draft, pushing to QuickBooks, and setting up billing cycles and
retainers are for owners and admins; members don't see those buttons. Over an API key or the
[MCP server](/mcp-server), generating, emailing and changing an invoice's
status are for owner and admin keys only.

**Who sees which invoices:** owners and admins see every invoice. Members
don't see an invoice that has a line on a
[confidential project](/concepts/clients-and-projects#confidential-projects)
they aren't on, because its lines and total would show that project's work.
For them it's left out of **Invoices**, the dashboard, and the client's page,
and its page and PDF read as not found.

## How to generate an invoice

1. Go to **Invoices** and click **New invoice** (or click it on a client's
   invoices card, which picks the client for you).
2. Pick the client. Consultainer lists everything for that client that's ready
   to bill:
   - Billable time not on an invoice yet. If
     [timesheet approval](/concepts/time-tracking#timesheet-approval) is on,
     only approved time is listed and a note says how many hours are still
     waiting.
   - Completed milestones not on an invoice yet.
   - Approved expenses not on an invoice yet.
3. Tick what to include. Everything starts ticked.
4. Check the details:
   - **Issue date** — today by default.
   - **Payment terms** and **Due date** — start from the
     [default terms](/concepts/clients-and-projects#payment-terms) and move
     together. Pick **Custom** to type a due date yourself.
   - **PO number** (optional), **Tax rate (%)** (starts at **Default tax
     rate** from Settings → General) and **Notes** (shown on the invoice).
5. Click **Generate invoice**. You land on the new **Draft**.

If the client has nothing to bill you'll see **Nothing to invoice** instead.

How items become lines:

- **Time** is grouped by project, person and rate: one line per group, for
  example *Website redesign — Ana Ruiz*, 12.5 × $150. The rate is the person's
  rate on the project, or the entry's rate override, plus any
  [overhead](/concepts/time-tracking#overhead).
- **Each milestone** becomes one line: *Project — Milestone: name*.
- **Each expense** becomes one line: *Project — Expense: description*.

Everything you include is marked as billed, so it can't be invoiced twice.
Invoices are in your organization's **Default currency**.

### Invoice numbers

Numbers are your **Invoice number prefix** (Settings → General, `INV` by
default), a dash, and a four-digit counter: `INV-0001`, `INV-0002`, and so on.
Numbers are never reused, so deleting a draft leaves a gap.

## How to edit a draft

While an invoice is a **Draft** you can change everything:

- **Line items** — edit a line's description, quantity or rate and click
  **Save** on that line; click the bin icon to remove it (time, milestones and
  expenses on it go back to unbilled). Editing a line doesn't change the time
  entries behind it.
- **Add a line** — fill in the empty row at the bottom (description, qty,
  rate) and click **Add**. Use this for a flat fee, a discount (negative rate)
  or anything else that isn't tracked as time, milestones or expenses.
- **Details** — **Issue date**, **Due date**, **Payment terms**, **PO
  number**, **Tax rate (%)** and **Notes**, then click **Save details**.
  Changing the terms here doesn't move the due date; set both.

Click **Download PDF** to see exactly what the client will get.

To throw a draft away, an owner or admin clicks **Delete**. Its time,
milestones and expenses become unbilled again.

Once an invoice is sent, it can't be edited.

### Status changes

An invoice only moves forward:

- **Draft → Sent** (it needs at least one line: *Add at least one line item
  before sending.*),
- **Sent → Paid**, once payments and applied credit cover the whole total
  (see [Getting paid](#getting-paid)). Deleting a payment recorded by mistake
  moves it back to **Sent**.
- **Draft or Sent → Void** (in the app, **Void** is on sent invoices; throw
  away a draft with **Delete** instead), as long as no payment or credit is
  recorded on it. Voiding puts the invoice's work back to unbilled; see
  [How to void an invoice](#how-to-void-an-invoice).

Nothing goes back to **Draft**, and a paid invoice can't be voided. Trying
anyway says, for example, *Only sent invoices can be marked paid.* or *Paid
invoices can't be voided.*

A sent invoice with part of it paid stays **Sent**, and shows as **Partly
paid · $X due** (the balance still owed) on the invoice, the invoices list,
the client and project pages, the client's share pages and the client invoice
page.

## How to send an invoice

You have three options, all for owners and admins. All of them change a
draft to **Sent**, which locks it, sends the *invoice sent* alert, and
[files the PDF](/integrations/files) if filing is set up.

### Email it from Consultainer (owners and admins)

This needs email set up on your instance (**Settings → Integrations**).

1. On the invoice, click **Email to client** (**Email again** on an invoice
   that's already sent).
2. Under **To**, untick anyone who shouldn't get it, and add anyone else
   (comma-separated, up to 20 in total). The defaults are the client's
   contacts with **Include on invoice emails** ticked, else its billing email,
   else its main email.
3. Optional: add a **Message**.
4. Click **Send invoice**.

The email has the invoice number, total, due date and a link to the
[client invoice page](#the-client-invoice-page). Replies go to your email
address. It shows under **Activity** as *Ana Ruiz emailed it to …* (with your name).

If email isn't set up, the dialog says so; use **Copy client link** instead.

### Send the link yourself

1. Click **Mark as sent** on the draft.
2. Click **Copy client link** and paste the link into your own email or
   message.

Opens through that link are tracked the same way.

### Mark as sent without sending anything

Click **Mark as sent** if you delivered the invoice some other way (for
example from [QuickBooks](/integrations/quickbooks)).

## How to schedule an invoice

Have a draft emailed on a date you pick, for example on the 1st of the month.
Owners and admins only; it needs email set up on your instance (**Settings →
Integrations**) and an email address for the client.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the draft">
    Go to **Invoices** and open the draft you want to send later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick when">
    Click **Schedule send**, then pick the **Date** and **Time (your time
    zone)**. It defaults to tomorrow at 9:00. The dialog shows who it goes to:
    the same people **Email to client** starts with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule it">
    Click **Schedule**. The invoice shows *Scheduled to send …* under its
    title, and **Invoices** shows *Sends …* next to its status. It also shows
    on the [calendar](/concepts/calendar).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To change the time, click **Reschedule**. To stop it, click **Cancel
schedule** on the invoice. Sending it yourself before then (**Email to
client** or **Mark as sent**) also means the schedule never fires, because
only drafts are sent.

### What happens when it's sent

The hourly job emails it within the hour after the time you picked, exactly
like **Email to client**, with no message. Replies go to whoever scheduled it.
It's marked sent, owners and admins get **An invoice is sent**, and
**Activity** shows *Emailed automatically to …*.

Its **Issue date** becomes the day it's sent, and the **Due date** moves by the
same number of days, so the payment terms still hold. For example, a Net 30
draft dated October 20 and scheduled for November 1 goes out dated November 1,
due December 1. Without this, a draft written well before it's sent would
arrive with part of its terms already used up.

### Check it worked

The invoice shows **Sent**, and **Activity** has *Emailed automatically to
…*. Until then it shows *Scheduled to send …*.

### Troubleshooting

If it can't be sent, nothing changes silently: the invoice stays a **Draft**
with its original dates and schedule, owners and admins get **A scheduled
invoice couldn't be sent** (Slack and email by default), and the invoice says
*Scheduled to send …, but it wasn't sent:* followed by the reason. It isn't
retried on its own; fix the cause, then **Reschedule** it or send it now.

| Reason shown | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Email isn't set up on this instance (Settings → Integrations).* | Set up [email](/integrations/email), then reschedule. |
| *… has no invoice contact, billing email or email address.* | Add an email on the client (or tick **Include on invoice emails** on a contact), then reschedule. |
| *The email couldn't be delivered.* | Check your email settings, then reschedule or click **Email to client**. |

When scheduling, *Pick a time in the future.* means the time you picked has
passed, and *Only draft invoices can be scheduled.* means it was sent or
voided already.

## The client invoice page

Every sent invoice has a private page at `https://<your-instance>/i/<token>`,
which is what **Email to client** and **Copy client link** give the client. No
login is needed. It shows:

- Your logo (or name), the invoice number, total, issue date and status.
  Once part of it is paid, the big figure is the amount still due, with the
  total and what's been paid underneath.
- **Due** date, and how many days overdue it is if it's late. A paid invoice
  says *Paid — thank you*; a void one says *This invoice was voided*.
- **Download PDF**, and the PDF itself on the page.
- **Pay now**, if [online payments](/integrations/payments) are set up and the
  invoice is unpaid. It charges the balance still due, not the original
  total. While a bank payment the client made is clearing, the
  page says *A bank payment for this invoice is processing* instead, and
  after paying the client sees a confirmation at the top of the page.
- **How to pay**: your [payment methods](/integrations/payments) for this
  client.

Drafts have no client page. The link keeps working after the invoice is paid
or voided.

## Tracking opens

Consultainer records three kinds of client activity and lists them under
**Activity** on the invoice, with a count (*Opened 3 times by the client*):

- **Client opened the email** — the email's tracking image loaded.
- **Client viewed the invoice** — the client invoice page was opened in a
  browser.
- **Client opened the PDF** — the PDF was opened or downloaded, from the
  invoice page or from a [client link](/concepts/clients-and-projects#client-links).

The first open sends an *invoice opened* alert (**A client opens an invoice**
under [Settings → Alerts](/concepts/alerts-and-audit)). Later opens alert
again only if a day has passed since the last alert.

Things to know:

- **Your own team's views don't count.** If you're signed in to Consultainer
  in the browser you open the link in, nothing is recorded. Open it in a
  private window to test it as a client would, and that view will count.
- **Email opens are best-effort.** Some mail apps block the tracking image
  (no open recorded), and some privacy features load it for everyone (an open
  recorded even if no one read it).
- Link scanners that fetch the page without running it don't count as views.

## Getting paid

An invoice's **balance due** is its total minus the payments recorded on it
and any [client credit](/concepts/deposits-and-credit) applied to it. It stays
**Sent** until the balance reaches zero, then becomes **Paid**. The **Summary**
card on the invoice shows **Total**, **Paid**, **Credit applied** and
**Balance due**, and the PDF shows the same rows ending in **Amount due**.

Recording payments, marking paid and deleting payments are for owners and
admins (in the app, over the API and over MCP).

### How to record a payment

Use this for part of the balance, or for a payment that arrived on another
day.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invoice">
    On a sent invoice, click **Record payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the payment">
    **Amount** starts at the balance due; change it for a part payment.
    Set **Received on** (today by default) and, optionally, **Method** (Wire,
    ACH, Check…), **Reference** (a check number, say) and a **Note**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Record payment**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Check it worked:** the payment is listed in the **Payments** card on the
invoice with the date, method and who recorded it. If it covered the rest of
the balance the invoice is **Paid** and you get the *invoice paid* alert;
otherwise it shows **Partly paid · $X due** and you get the *part payment
received* alert (**A part payment is received** under Settings → Alerts).

You can't record more than is due: *That's more than the $600.00 still due on
this invoice.* (Online payments are the exception: if a client pays through
Stripe or Mercury while you were recording a payment by hand, the extra is
kept as [client credit](/concepts/deposits-and-credit).)

### How to mark an invoice paid

**Mark as paid** is a shortcut that records one payment for the whole balance
due, dated today.

1. On a sent invoice, click **Mark as paid**. The dialog says how much it will
   record.
2. Optional: enter the **Payment method** (for example Wire, ACH, Check).
3. Click **Mark as paid**.

This sends the *invoice paid* alert and, if filing is set up, updates the
filed PDF. Invoices paid online through Stripe or Mercury get their payment
recorded automatically once it has gone through (a bank payment through
Stripe takes a few business days); see [Payments](/integrations/payments).

### How to delete a payment recorded by mistake

1. In the invoice's **Payments** card, click the bin icon next to the payment.
2. Confirm.

The payment is removed, the balance goes back up, and a paid invoice returns
to **Sent**. The deletion is in the [audit log](/concepts/alerts-and-audit)
(*deleted payment*), along with who recorded it in the first place. Deleting
a Stripe, Mercury or QuickBooks payment here doesn't refund or cancel
anything there.

A payment on a [deposit invoice](/concepts/deposits-and-credit) can't be
deleted once its credit has been spent: *Credit from this payment has already
been applied to other invoices. Remove that credit from them first.*

### Payments before this version

Invoices that were already **Paid** when you upgraded got one payment for
their full total, dated when they were marked paid.

### Overdue invoices

A sent invoice is overdue the day after its due date. Once a day, Consultainer
sends one *invoice overdue* alert per newly overdue invoice (**An invoice
becomes overdue** under Settings → Alerts). Overdue invoices show the days
overdue in the app and on the client page.

### Overdue reminders

Consultainer can email clients reminders about overdue invoices. It's off
until you turn it on, and needs email set up on your instance.

1. Go to **Settings → Payments → Overdue reminders** (owners and admins).
2. In **Days overdue**, list when to send a reminder, for example `3, 7, 14`
   (up to 10 whole numbers from 1 to 365). Leave it empty to turn reminders
   off.
3. Click **Save**.

How reminders work:

- They're checked once a day. Each reminder goes to the same people as
  **Email to client** would by default, with the subject *Reminder: invoice
  INV-0042 from Your Org is overdue* and a link to the invoice page.
- Each threshold is sent once per invoice. If an invoice is already past
  several thresholds when you turn reminders on, only the latest one goes
  out, so clients don't get a backlog.
- They stop when the invoice is paid or voided. A reminder for a partly paid
  invoice asks for the balance due.
- Each one shows under **Activity** as *Reminder (7 days overdue) sent to …*.

To stop reminders for one client, open the client, click **Edit**, untick
**Email overdue reminders**, and save.

## How to void an invoice

Owners and admins can void a sent invoice, as long as nothing has been paid
or credited on it. Once a payment or credit is recorded, **Void** isn't
offered and the **Payments** card says why: *This invoice has payments or
credit recorded, so it can't be voided. Delete them first if they were a
mistake, or issue a credit note for the rest of the balance.* To write off the
rest of a partly paid invoice, issue a
[credit note](/concepts/deposits-and-credit#credit-notes) for it and apply it.

1. On the sent invoice, click **Void**.
2. Read the confirmation and click **OK**. Voiding can't be undone.

What happens:

- The invoice is marked **Void**. It stays in your list (and its client page
  says it was voided) but stops counting as invoiced or outstanding.
- Its time entries, completed milestones and approved expenses go back to
  **unbilled**. They show up on **New invoice** again, so you can bill them on
  a corrected invoice, and a [billing cycle](#billing-cycles) picks them up on
  its next run.
- The voided invoice keeps its lines and total as a record of what was sent.
- If filing is set up, the filed PDF is replaced with the void version.

Voiding doesn't create a replacement invoice. Retainers aren't re-run either:
a voided retainer invoice isn't generated again, so if you still want to bill
that period, create the invoice by hand.

If the invoice is still a draft, **Delete** it instead (that also releases its
work).

## Billing cycles

A billing cycle invoices a client on a schedule for everything unbilled, so
you don't have to remember to. Owners and admins set it up on the client page.

### How to set up a billing cycle

1. Open the client and find the **Billing cycle** card.
2. Click **Set up billing cycle**.
3. Pick a **Frequency**: Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Twice a month (1st & 15th),
   Monthly, Quarterly or Yearly.
4. Set the **First invoice date**.
5. Check the **Payment terms** (they start from the client's default).
6. Optional: tick **Send automatically (emails the invoice to the client)
   instead of leaving a draft to review** (see
   [Sending automatically](#sending-automatically)).
7. Click **Start billing cycle**.

What each run does:

- It invoices all of the client's billable time, completed milestones and
  approved expenses dated **before** the run date, on one invoice, with the
  note *Services through* the day before. Time waiting for timesheet approval
  waits for the next run.
- It uses the cycle's payment terms and your default tax rate.
- If there's nothing to bill, it creates nothing and **Last run** says
  *Nothing unbilled — no invoice created*.
- Without **Send automatically**, the invoice is left as a draft and owners
  and admins get the **A recurring invoice or billing cycle needs review**
  alert to review and send it. With it, the invoice is emailed to the client
  and marked sent (see [Sending automatically](#sending-automatically)).
- If the server was down over several run dates, the next run bills once, up
  to the latest date, then carries on.
- If a run fails, owners and admins are notified and the work rolls into the
  next run.

Use **Edit** to change the cycle, the pause button to pause or resume it, and
the bin icon to stop it. Invoices already generated aren't affected.


## Retainers

A retainer bills a fixed amount on a schedule (a monthly retainer, say),
whatever time is logged. Owners and admins set them up on the client page; a
client can have several.

### How to set up a retainer

1. Open the client and find the **Retainers** card.
2. Click **New retainer**.
3. Enter a **Description** (it becomes the invoice line, for example *Monthly
   retainer*) and the **Amount**.
4. Pick a **Frequency**.
5. Optional: enter **Retainer hours** if the amount covers a block of hours.
   The client's invoices card then shows hours billed against hours logged.
6. Set the **First invoice date** and **Due (days after issue)** (30 by
   default).
7. Optional: tick **Send automatically (emails the invoice to the client)
   instead of leaving a draft to review** (see
   [Sending automatically](#sending-automatically)). The retainer then shows
   *Sends automatically* instead of *Drafts for review*.
8. Click **Create schedule**.

Each run creates an invoice with one line for the amount and no tax. A draft
raises the **A recurring invoice or billing cycle needs review** alert; with
**Send automatically**, the invoice is emailed to the client and marked sent.
Use the pause and bin buttons on the retainer to pause, resume or delete it;
invoices already generated aren't affected.

## Sending automatically

Billing cycles and retainers with **Send automatically** ticked email each
invoice to the client as soon as it's generated, the same way
**Email to client** does:

- It goes to the client's contacts with **Include on invoice emails** ticked,
  else its billing email, else its main email (the same people the
  **Email to client** dialog starts with), with no message.
- Replies go to the organization's owner.
- The invoice is marked sent, owners and admins get the **An invoice is
  sent** alert, and the PDF is filed if filing is set up.
- It shows under **Activity** as *Emailed automatically to …*, opens are
  [tracked](#tracking-opens), and [overdue reminders](#overdue-reminders) go
  out as for any emailed invoice.

If it can't be emailed — email isn't set up on your instance, the client has
no email address, or delivery failed — the invoice is still marked sent, and
owners and admins get the **A recurring invoice or billing cycle needs
review** alert saying why, for example *…marked sent, but it wasn't emailed:
Acme has no invoice contact, billing email or email address.* Fix the cause,
then send it yourself with **Email again** or **Copy client link**.

## When scheduled invoicing runs

Billing cycles, retainers, overdue alerts and overdue reminders all run in one
daily job, driven by the `scheduler` service in `docker-compose.yml`. It runs
about a minute after the scheduler starts and then every 24 hours, so the
time of day depends on when the stack was last started. A run date means
"the first daily run on or after that date". If the scheduler isn't running
(or `CRON_SECRET` doesn't match), nothing scheduled happens; see
[Self-hosting](/self-hosting).

## PDFs, filing and QuickBooks

- **Download PDF** on any invoice opens its PDF.
- If filing is set up, each invoice's PDF is saved to your Drive, Dropbox or
  OneDrive folder (under the client's name, in *Invoices*) when it's sent,
  and replaced with the paid (or void) version when that changes, and the invoice
  shows **Filed to …**. See [Documents & files](/integrations/files).
- If QuickBooks is connected, you can push an invoice to QuickBooks and sync
  its status back. See [QuickBooks](/integrations/quickbooks).

## Over MCP

`list_invoices` (with `amountPaid`, `creditApplied` and `balanceDue`),
`generate_invoice`, `email_invoice`, `schedule_invoice_send`, `get_invoice_activity`,
`mark_invoice_sent`, `mark_invoice_paid` (records a payment for the whole
balance), `record_invoice_payment`, `list_invoice_payments` and
`void_invoice`. Generating, emailing, scheduling, recording payments and the
three status tools need an owner or admin key. See the [MCP server](/mcp-server).
