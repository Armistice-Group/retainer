## Generating an invoice

From **Invoices → New**, pick a client. Consultainer shows everything for
that client currently eligible to bill:

- Unbilled, billable time entries
- Completed, unbilled milestones
- Approved, unbilled expenses

Select what to include, set the issue date, payment terms, tax rate, PO
number, and notes, then generate. Consultainer groups hourly time by
project and person into line items automatically; milestones and expenses
each become their own line item.

## Payment terms

Due on receipt, Net 15/30/45/60/90, or Custom. Selecting a term on the
new-invoice form auto-computes the due date from the issue date; Custom
leaves the due date for you to set manually.

## Editing a draft

While an invoice is in **Draft**, its line items, issue date, due date, tax
rate, payment terms, PO number, and notes are all editable. Once it's marked
**Sent**, line items lock.

## Statuses

`Draft → Sent → Paid`, or `Void` at any point before Paid. Marking an
invoice Paid can record a payment method (e.g. Wire, ACH, Check).

## PDF and QuickBooks

Every invoice can be downloaded as a PDF. If QuickBooks is connected, a
draft or sent invoice can be pushed to QuickBooks directly, and its
paid/sent status can be pulled back into Consultainer with a manual sync
(QuickBooks doesn't push status changes to Consultainer automatically).

See [QuickBooks integration](/integrations/quickbooks) for setup.
