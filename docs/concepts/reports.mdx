**Reports** answers four questions: who's busy on billable work, which
projects make money, what's done but not yet invoiced, and who owes you.
Owners and admins see **Reports** in the sidebar; members don't.

## How to run a report

1. Click **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Pick a range: **This month**, **Last month**, **This quarter**, **Year to
   date** or **Last 12 months**. For any other range, set the two dates and
   click **Apply**.
3. Read the totals at the top (**Hours logged**, **Invoiced**, **Profit**,
   **Received**, **Outstanding**) and the four sections below.
4. Click **CSV** on a section to download it. The file opens in Excel, Numbers
   or Google Sheets.

## What each section shows

| Section | What it shows | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Utilization** | Per person: hours, billable hours, billable hours as a share of capacity (8 hours per weekday in the range), the billable value of their time, and what it cost you. | The range you picked |
| **Profit by project** | Invoiced (sent or paid invoices issued in the range) minus labor cost and approved expenses in the range, with margin. | The range you picked |
| **Unbilled work** | Per client: billable time, completed milestones and approved expenses that aren't on an invoice yet. | All time, as of now |
| **Receivables** | Per client: the balance still due on sent invoices (total minus part payments and applied credit) by days overdue (current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+). | As of now |

Draft and void invoices never count as invoiced or outstanding.
[Deposit invoices](/concepts/deposits-and-credit) don't count as invoiced
(the invoice for the work does).

**Received** is cash in: the payments recorded with a received date in the
range, part payments included, in your default currency (other currencies
are listed under it). Applied credit isn't counted, because the deposit or
payment it came from already was. **Outstanding** is the total balance due
from **Receivables**.

## How to set costs

Profit and utilization cost need to know what an hour of each person costs
you.

1. Go to **Settings → Members** (owners and admins).
2. In the **Cost** field next to each person, enter what you pay them per
   hour, or an hourly equivalent of their salary.

Only owners and admins see costs. If someone logged time in the range without
a cost set, the page says so, their labor counts as zero, and the affected
labor figures are marked with `*`.

The **Rate** field next to **Cost** is the person's default bill rate for new
projects. Reports don't use it: billable value always comes from each
person's rate on the project (or an entry's rate override).

## Weekly digest

The same numbers feed the
[weekly digest](/concepts/alerts-and-audit#weekly-digest), a Monday summary sent to whoever
your alert settings name for it. Its **Open Reports** button opens this page
for the week the digest covers.
