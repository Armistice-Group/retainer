title: Reports
description: Utilization, profit by project, unbilled work, and receivables
---

Owners and admins get **Reports** in the sidebar. Pick a range — this month,
last month, this quarter, year to date, the last 12 months, or any dates —
and every section can be downloaded as CSV.

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Utilization** | Per person: hours, billable hours, billable share of capacity (8 hours per weekday in the range), the billable value of their time, and what it cost. |
| **Profit by project** | Invoiced (sent or paid invoices issued in the range) minus labor cost and approved expenses in the range, with margin. |
| **Unbilled work** | Per client, as of now: billable time, completed milestones and approved expenses not on an invoice yet. |
| **Receivables** | Per client, as of now: sent, unpaid invoices by days overdue (current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+). |

## Costs

Profit needs to know what an hour of each person's time costs you. Set it
under **Settings → Members** (the **Cost** field next to each person — what
you pay them per hour, or an hourly equivalent of their salary). Only owners
and admins see it. If time was logged by someone without a cost, the page
says so and their labor counts as zero.

The same numbers feed the [weekly digest](/concepts/alerts-and-audit#weekly-digest).
