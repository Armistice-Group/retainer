**Calendar** in the sidebar shows, on one month, everything with a date that
you're allowed to see. The dashboard's **Upcoming** card shows the next two
weeks of it: open deadlines, your meetings that haven't happened yet, and
upcoming bookings.

**Who can do this:** everyone. What's on it depends on your role and on which
projects you can see (see [What's on it](#whats-on-it)).

## What's on it

Each kind of item has its own icon and colour, and links to where you can act
on it.

| Item | Shows | Who sees it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Meetings** | Your meetings from the calendars you connected under **Profile → Calendars**, at their time: the last two weeks and the next 30 days. Ones still to come are marked **Upcoming**; ones that have ended are **To sort**, **Logged** or **Ignored** as in **Time → Meetings**. | Only you. Nobody sees anyone else's meetings. |
| **Bookings** | Meetings booked through [Cal.com or Calendly](/integrations/scheduling), at their time, with who booked (a draft client is labelled) and **Cancelled** or **No-show** when they are. A booking that's also in your own calendar shows once, as your meeting. | Owners and admins: every booking. Others: the ones they host. |
| **Tasks** | Due dates of your tasks, or everyone's (**Everyone's tasks**). Overdue ones are red; done ones are struck through. | Anyone, for projects they can see. |
| **Milestones & deliverables** | Due dates of [milestones and deliverables](/concepts/milestones). | Anyone, for projects they can see. |
| **Project dates** | A project's start and end dates (set with **Edit** on the project). | Anyone, for projects they can see. |
| **Invoices due** | Due dates of sent, unpaid invoices. | Owners and admins. |
| **Scheduled sends** | Drafts [scheduled to send](/concepts/invoicing#how-to-schedule-an-invoice), at their time. One that failed is red. | Owners and admins. |
| **Recurring invoices** | The next run of each active [retainer](/concepts/invoicing#retainers). | Owners and admins. |
| **Billing cycles** | When each client's [billing cycle](/concepts/invoicing#billing-cycles) next closes and invoices its unbilled work. | Owners and admins. |
| **Estimate expiries** | The last day a draft or sent [estimate](/concepts/estimates) can be accepted. | Owners and admins. |

Nothing from a [confidential project](/concepts/clients-and-projects#confidential-projects)
you aren't on appears, and archived projects are left out.

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Calendar">
    Click **Calendar** in the sidebar. It opens on this month, with today
    circled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move between months">
    Use the arrows next to the month name; **Today** brings you back. The
    month is in the address (`?month=2026-11`), so you can bookmark or share
    a view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch views">
    **Month** is a grid; a day with more than four items shows **+N more**,
    which opens that day in **Agenda**. **Agenda** lists every day of the
    month that has something on it, with the client and project of each item.
    On a phone, the month shows only days with something on them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter">
    Pick a client or a project, and **My tasks** or **Everyone's tasks**. Click
    the chips (**Meetings**, **Tasks**, …) to hide or show each kind.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Meeting times are shown in your browser's time zone (the page says which, at
the bottom). Due dates are whole days and are the same everywhere.

## Due dates on tasks

Give a task a **Due date** when you add it, or with the pencil (**Edit task**)
later. It shows on the task's row and sheet: red once it's past, amber the day
before and on the day. See [Tasks](/concepts/tasks#due-dates).

## Deadline reminders

The daily job (see [Self-hosting → Scheduled jobs](/self-hosting#scheduled-jobs))
sends these once per due date. Changing the due date re-arms them.

| Reminder | Who gets it |
| --- | --- |
| **A task is due tomorrow** | The task's assignee, in the app and by email to their own address. |
| **A task or milestone is overdue**, for a task | The task's assignee, in the app and by email to them. |
| **A task or milestone is overdue**, for a milestone or deliverable | Owners, admins and the people on the project, in the app; Slack and the alert email list, per **Settings → Alerts**. |

Done tasks, completed milestones, unassigned tasks and projects that are
completed or archived don't get reminders. Owners and admins choose Slack and
email for each under **Settings → Alerts** (see
[Alerts](/concepts/alerts-and-audit#the-alerts)); the in-app notification is
always sent. On a confidential project, Slack and email leave out the task,
milestone and project names.

## Subscribe in your calendar app

Get your deadlines in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook with a
private address only you have.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the address">
    Go to **Profile → Subscribe in your calendar app** and click **Create
    calendar address**. Copy it: it's shown only once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to your calendar app">
    - **Google Calendar:** **Other calendars → + → From URL**, paste it, then
      **Add calendar**.
    - **Apple Calendar:** **File → New Calendar Subscription**, paste it.
    - **Outlook:** **Add calendar → Subscribe from web**, paste it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The feed has all-day events for your tasks' due dates, milestones and
deliverables, and project start and end dates, plus (for owners and admins)
invoice due dates, scheduled sends, recurring invoice runs, billing cycle
closes and estimate expiries: from two months back to a year ahead, for the
organization you had open when you created it. Done items end in *(done)*.
Each event links back to the app. Meetings are never in it; they're in your
calendar already.

It's worked out from your access whenever it's read, so if you leave a
project or the organization, its items drop out (within a few minutes). Only
a hash of the address is stored.

**Make a new address** replaces it: the old one stops working at once.
**Turn off** removes it. Do either if the address might have leaked: anyone
with it can read your deadlines.

### Check it worked

Your calendar app shows a calendar named *Consultainer — your organization*,
and **Profile** says *last read by a calendar app* with a date. Opening the
address in a browser shows (or downloads) a calendar file that starts with
`BEGIN:VCALENDAR`.

### Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The address says Not found">
    It was replaced (**Make a new address**) or turned off, or you're no longer
    in the organization. Create a new one and update your calendar app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A new due date doesn't show in my calendar app yet">
    Calendar apps fetch subscriptions on their own schedule: Google Calendar can
    take several hours, Apple Calendar uses the refresh interval you picked.
    The app also caches each feed for a few minutes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The calendar app can't reach the address">
    It has to reach your instance from the internet. If something in front of
    the app requires a login, let `/api/calendar/*` through (see
    [Self-hosting](/self-hosting#behind-an-authenticating-proxy)).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Over the API and MCP

Tasks take a `dueDate` (`YYYY-MM-DD`) when created or updated, over the
[REST API](/api-reference/tasks) and the [MCP server](/mcp-server)
(`create_task`, `update_task`; `list_my_tasks` lists the soonest due first).
An invalid date gets a 422 (API) or an error (MCP). Owners and admins can
schedule invoices with `schedule_invoice_send`.
