Every project has a task list, and **Tasks** in the sidebar shows them across
all your projects, grouped by project. Filter by **Mine / Everyone** and
**Open / Done / All**; in-progress tasks come first. **New task** lets you pick
the project.

## The task sheet

Click a task (on its project page or under Tasks) to open its sheet:

- **Status** and **assignee** — change them right there. Assigning someone
  notifies them.
- **Hours** — logged against the task, next to its estimate. Task lists show
  hours in red once they pass the estimate, and the assignee and admins are
  alerted.
- **Linear** — the issue key (e.g. `RING-12`) linking to it, for tasks synced
  with [Linear](/integrations/linear).
- **Time** — **Start timer** (or **Stop timer** when it's running on this
  task) and **Log time**, plus the entries logged so far.
- **Comments** — see below.

The sheet's URL (`?task=…`) can be shared; it opens straight to the task.

## Timers

Every task row has a play button: it starts a timer on that task, stopping
and logging whatever timer was running, and turns into a stop button while
it's the running one. The running timer also shows in the top bar. Over MCP:
`start_timer`, `stop_timer`, `get_active_timer`.

## Comments

Comments are internal by default — clients never see them unless you choose
to share one.

- **@mentions** — type `@` to pick someone who can see the project. They're
  notified directly. (Mentions of people who can't see a confidential project
  are ignored.)
- **Watchers** — the assignee and watchers are notified of new comments.
  Commenting or being mentioned makes you a watcher; **Watch / Unwatch** on
  the sheet changes it.
- **Linear** — on a task linked to a Linear issue, comments are posted there
  too (untick **Also post to Linear** to keep one internal), and comments
  made in Linear show up here marked **From Linear**.
- **Share with client** — when the project's client link shows tasks, tick
  this to show a comment there. Shared comments are badged **Client can see**,
  and their author or an admin can change it later.

Authors can delete their own comments; owners and admins can delete any. A
copy already posted to Linear stays there.
