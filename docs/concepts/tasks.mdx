title: Tasks
description: Add and assign tasks, track time on them, and discuss them with your team

Tasks break a project into pieces of work you can assign, time and discuss.
Every project has a task list on its page, and **Tasks** in the sidebar shows
tasks across all the projects you can see. Anyone who can see a project can
add, edit, assign and delete its tasks. A task can only be assigned to
someone who can see its project too: on a
[confidential project](/concepts/clients-and-projects#confidential-projects),
that's the people on it, plus owners and admins.

## How to add a task

1. Open the project and click **Add task** on the **Tasks** card, or click
   **New task** under **Tasks** and pick the **Project**.
2. Enter a **Title** and, optionally, a **Description**.
3. Optional: set a **Due date** (see [Due dates](#due-dates)).
4. Optional: set **Estimated hours**. You're alerted when logged time passes
   it (see [Estimates](#estimates)).
5. Optional: pick an **Assignee**. They're notified.
6. Click **Add task**.

<Note>
A project needs at least one person on its team before it can have tasks. If
the **Tasks** card says **Add a team member first**, an owner or admin adds
someone under **Team & bill rates** (see
[Clients & Projects](/concepts/clients-and-projects#how-to-add-people-and-set-bill-rates)).
</Note>

## How to find your tasks

Go to **Tasks** in the sidebar. Tasks are grouped by project.

- **Mine** shows tasks assigned to you; **Everyone** shows all tasks.
- **Open** shows tasks that aren't done; **Done** and **All** do what they
  say.
- **Overdue** shows tasks past their due date that aren't done; **Due this
  week** shows tasks due from today through Sunday; **Any date** shows all.
- **By due date** puts the soonest due first (tasks without one last), and
  the projects with the soonest deadline first; **By status** puts tasks in
  progress first.

## How to change status or assignee

A task is **To do**, **In progress** or **Done**.

- **On the project page:** click a task's status badge to move it to the next
  one (To do → In progress → Done → To do). Click the assignee name to pick
  someone else or **Unassigned**.
- **On the task sheet:** use the **Status** and **Assignee** menus.

Assigning someone notifies them. If the project is linked to Linear with
changes sent back, status and assignee changes go to the issue too.

To edit a task's title, description, due date or estimate, click the pencil
on its row (**Edit task**). To delete it, click the bin icon and confirm. Deleting a
task also deletes its comments. Time logged on it is kept, just no longer
linked to a task.

If you pick someone who can't see the project, you'll get *That person can't
be assigned tasks on this project.* An owner or admin can add them to the
project's team first.

## The task sheet

Click a task's title (on its project page or under **Tasks**) to open its
sheet. It shows:

- **Status** and **Assignee**, which you can change right there.
- **Due**: the due date, or *No due date*.
- **Hours** logged on the task next to its estimate, marked **over estimate**
  once it passes.
- **Linear**: the issue key (for example `RING-12`) and **Open issue**, for a
  task synced with [Linear](/integrations/linear).
- **Time**: **Start timer** / **Stop timer**, **Log time**, and every entry
  logged on the task.
- **Comments**.

The page URL gets `?task=…` while the sheet is open. Copy it to send someone
straight to the task.

## Due dates

A task's due date shows on its row (on the project page and under **Tasks**)
and on its sheet: *Due Aug 14*, amber on the day before and the day itself
(*Due tomorrow*, *Due today*), red once it's past. Done tasks stay grey. Due
dates are whole days.

- The assignee is reminded the day before (**A task is due tomorrow**) and
  once it's overdue (**A task or milestone is overdue**), in the app and by
  email. Changing the date re-arms both. See
  [Deadline reminders](/concepts/calendar#deadline-reminders).
- Due dates show on the [calendar](/concepts/calendar) and the dashboard's
  **Upcoming** card, and in your [calendar subscription](/concepts/calendar#subscribe-in-your-calendar-app).
- On a project synced with [Linear](/integrations/linear), the due date syncs
  both ways with the issue's due date.

If the date isn't valid you'll see *That date doesn't exist.* (for example
February 30).

## How to track time on a task

- **Timer:** click the play button on the task's row, or **Start timer** on
  its sheet. Click the same button (now a stop button) or **Stop** in the top
  bar to stop it; the time is logged against the task.
- **By hand:** click **Log time** on the sheet. The project and task are
  filled in for you.

Only one timer runs at a time: starting one on another task stops the running
one and logs its time first. Timer details are in
[Time Tracking](/concepts/time-tracking#how-to-use-the-timer).

### Estimates

When the hours logged on a task first pass its **Estimated hours**, owners,
admins and the task's assignee get an alert (*A project nears its budget or a
task passes its estimate* under [Settings → Alerts](/concepts/alerts-and-audit)).
Hours on the task list turn red. If hours drop back under, or you raise the
estimate, the alert can fire again.

## Comments

Comments are internal: the client never sees them unless you share a specific
one.

### How to comment and @mention someone

1. Open the task sheet.
2. Type in **Add a comment…** at the bottom. Type `@` and pick a name to
   mention someone. Only people who can see the project are offered.
3. Click **Comment** (or press Ctrl+Enter / Cmd+Enter).

Who gets notified:

- **People you @mention** get a "mentioned you" notification.
- **The assignee and the task's watchers** get a "commented on" notification.
- You're never notified about your own comment.

Commenting on a task, or being mentioned on it, makes you a watcher.

### How to watch or stop watching a task

On the task sheet, click **Watch** next to **Comments** to be notified of new
comments, or **Unwatch** to stop. The assignee is always notified and sees
**Notified as assignee** instead.

### How to share a comment with the client

This works once the project has a client link with **Show tasks** turned on
(see [Client links](/concepts/clients-and-projects#how-to-share-a-project-with-the-client)).

1. Write your comment and tick **Share with client (shown on the project's
   client link)**.
2. Click **Comment**.

The comment is badged **Client can see** and appears under its task on the
client link. To change an existing comment, hover over it and click **Share
with client** or **Hide from client**. The comment's author, owners and admins
can do this.

### Comments and Linear

On a task linked to a Linear issue, your comment is also posted on the issue.
Untick **Also post to Linear** before you click **Comment** to keep it in
Consultainer only. Comments made in Linear show up on the task marked **From
Linear**. See [Linear](/integrations/linear).

### Deleting comments

Hover over a comment and click the bin icon. Authors can delete their own
comments; owners and admins can delete any. A copy already posted to Linear
stays there.

## Over MCP

AI agents connected through the [MCP server](/mcp-server) can use
`list_my_tasks` (soonest due first), `create_task` and `update_task` (both
take a `dueDate`, `YYYY-MM-DD`; `null` clears it on `update_task`), `update_task_status`,
`list_task_comments`, `add_task_comment`, `delete_task_comment`, `watch_task`,
`start_timer` and `stop_timer`.
