A **client** is the company or person you consult for. A **project** is one
engagement for a client, with its own team, bill rates and billing type. Time,
tasks, milestones and expenses all hang off a project; invoices go to a client.

Anyone in your organization can create and edit clients and projects.
Deleting them, managing a project's team and bill rates, and a few other
things are for owners and admins only, and are marked below.

## How to add a client

1. Go to **Clients** and click **Add client** (or **Import** to bring in a CSV
   of clients and projects — see [Importing](#how-to-import-clients-and-projects)).
2. Fill in **Company name**. Everything else is optional:
   - **Contact** — the company's general email, phone and address.
   - **Billing** — **Billing email** and **Billing address**, if invoices go
     somewhere other than the contact details. Leave them blank to use the
     contact details.
   - **Payment terms** — leave it on **Organization default** unless this
     client has its own terms. See [Payment terms](#payment-terms).
   - **Email overdue reminders** — on by default. Untick it to never send this
     client [overdue reminders](/concepts/invoicing#overdue-reminders).
3. Click **Create client**. You land on the client page, with a prompt to add
   the people you work with there.

To change any of this later, click **Edit** on the client page. Set **Status**
to **Inactive** for a client you no longer work with.

<Warning>
**Delete** on a client page (owners and admins) removes the client together
with all of its projects, time entries **and invoices**, including paid ones.
It can't be undone. To retire a client, set its status to **Inactive** instead.
</Warning>

## How to add contacts

Contacts are the people you work with at the client.

1. On the client page, click **Add contact** (in **Points of contact**).
2. Enter **Name** and, optionally, **Title**, **Role**, **Email** and **Phone**.
3. Tick **Primary contact** for your main contact. Only one contact can be
   primary; ticking it on someone else moves it.
4. Tick **Include on invoice emails** for everyone who should get invoices.
5. Click **Add contact**.

Who gets invoice emails (including ones
[sent automatically](/concepts/invoicing#sending-automatically) by a billing
cycle or retainer) and overdue reminders:

1. Every contact with **Include on invoice emails** ticked (and an email).
2. If there are none, the client's **Billing email**.
3. If that's blank too, the client's main **Email**.

The client page's **Billing** card shows who that is right now. The primary
contact is also who gets the email asking them to approve a contractor (see
[Team & bill rates](#how-to-add-people-and-set-bill-rates)).

## How to add a project

1. Click **New project** on the client page (the client is picked for you) or
   under **Projects**.
2. Fill in **Project name** and **Client**.
3. Pick a **Billing type** (see [Billing types](#billing-types)). For **Flat
   fee**, enter the **Flat fee amount**.
4. Optionally set **Budget (hours)**, **Payment terms**, **Start date** and
   **End date**, and tick **Confidential** (see
   [Confidential projects](#confidential-projects)).
5. Click **Create project**.

**Status** (Active, On hold, Completed, Archived) is for your own tracking; it
doesn't stop anyone logging time.

<Warning>
**Delete** on a project page (owners and admins) removes the project and all
of its time entries,
tasks, milestones and expenses. Set the status to **Completed** or
**Archived** instead if you might need the history.
</Warning>

### Billing types

| Type | How it gets invoiced |
| --- | --- |
| **Hourly** | From logged, billable time at each person's rate on the project. |
| **Milestone-based** | Each [milestone](/concepts/milestones) is billed for its amount once it's marked complete. Time is still tracked, and billable time can still be invoiced. |
| **Flat fee** | The amount is shown on the project's **Billing** card and client link. It is **not** added to invoices for you: bill it by adding a line to a draft invoice (see [Editing a draft](/concepts/invoicing#how-to-edit-a-draft)) or with a [retainer](/concepts/invoicing#retainers). |

Every project can also have [expenses](/concepts/expenses), whatever its
billing type.

## How to add people and set bill rates

**Who can do this:** owners and admins. Members see the project's team and
rates but can't change them.

Each person on a project has their own hourly rate there, independent of any
other project. Billable time is invoiced at that rate unless an owner or admin
sets a **Rate override** on the entry.

The rate someone starts at comes from their default rate:

1. Their own **Rate** on **Settings → Members**, if set.
2. Otherwise the organization's **Default bill rate** on **Settings →
   General**.
3. Otherwise **0**.

Changing a default never changes rates on projects people are already on.

To add someone:

1. On the project page, click **Add team member** (in **Team & bill rates**).
2. Pick the **Team member**. Their **Hourly rate** is filled in from their
   default rate; change it for this project if you need to, and check the
   **Currency**.
3. If they're a contractor, you can tick **Email the client to approve this
   contractor before they start**. The client's primary contact is emailed a
   link to approve or reject them, and the person shows as **Pending** until
   they answer. Owners and admins can also copy that link with **Review link**.
   Approval is a record for you and the client; it doesn't block anyone from
   logging time.
4. Click **Add to project**. They're notified that they were added.

To change a rate, click the pencil next to it (**Edit rate**). Rate changes
apply to time not yet invoiced. To take someone off, click the bin icon.

If anyone on an hourly project is at **$0/hr**, owners and admins see a note
under the team list: their billable time would invoice at zero. Fix it with
the pencil, and set default rates so new projects don't start at zero.

<Note>
Tasks can only be added once someone is on the project. In a one-person
organization you're added to every project automatically, at your default
rate. The same happens when a member creates a confidential project (so they
can still see it).
</Note>

<Note>
Invoices are always in your organization's **Default currency** (Settings →
General). The currency next to a rate is a label; amounts aren't converted.
</Note>

## Budgets

A project can have a **Budget (hours)**: a not-to-exceed cap on hours.

- The project's **Billing** card shows hours logged against the budget, and
  changes color at 80% (showing hours remaining) and again once it's over (showing hours over). The project's client link shows the same bar.
- Every hour logged on the project counts, billable or not.
- Owners and admins get a **budget alert** the first time the project reaches
  80% and again at 100%, through whatever channels are on for *A project nears
  its budget or a task passes its estimate* under
  [Settings → Alerts](/concepts/alerts-and-audit).
- A task with **Estimated hours** works the same way: the first time its
  logged hours pass the estimate, owners, admins and the task's assignee are
  alerted.
- An alert can fire again if hours drop back under (an entry is deleted) or
  the budget or estimate is raised and then reached again.

To set or change a budget, click **Edit** on the project page and fill in
**Budget (hours)**.

## Payment terms

Payment terms decide an invoice's due date: **Due on receipt**, **Net 15**,
**Net 30**, **Net 45**, **Net 60** or **Net 90**. You can set a default at
three levels; the most specific one wins:

1. **Project** — **Payment terms** on the project's edit page. Used only when
   every item on the invoice comes from that one project.
2. **Client** — **Payment terms** on the client's edit page.
3. **Organization** — **Default payment terms** under **Settings → General**
   (Net 30 unless you change it).

Each level shows what it falls back to: a client's form says **Organization
default (Net 30)**, a project's says **Same as client (Net 30)**.

On **New invoice**, the terms start from that default and the due date
follows. You can still pick other terms, or **Custom** to type a due date
yourself, for that one invoice. The REST API and MCP server use the same
defaults when you don't pass terms or a due date. A
[billing cycle](/concepts/invoicing#billing-cycles) has its own terms, which
start from the client's.

## Confidential projects

Tick **Confidential** on a project to make it need-to-know, for example under
an NDA.

- **Who can see it:** owners, admins, and the people on its **Team & bill
  rates** list. Everyone else doesn't see it anywhere: not in project lists, the client page,
  tasks, time or invoice generation. Its tasks can only be assigned to people
  who can see it.
- **Invoices:** a member who isn't on the project doesn't see any invoice
  with a line for it either (not in **Invoices**, on the dashboard or the
  client page, and its page and PDF read as not found), because the
  invoice's lines and total would show the project's work.
- If a member (not an admin) creates or marks a project confidential, they're
  added to its team automatically so they don't lock themselves out.
- **@mentions** on its tasks only reach people who can see it.
- Its documents are hidden from people who can't see the project.
- The client's [all-projects client link](#client-links) leaves it out
  entirely, along with any invoice that includes its work.

## Documents

Clients and projects each have a **Documents** card for contracts, SOWs,
proposals, reports, W-9s and reference docs. A client's card also lists its
projects' documents, labelled with the project.

### How to add a document

1. On the client or project page, click **Add** on the **Documents** card.
2. Choose **Link** or **Upload**:
   - **Link** — paste a Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive / SharePoint, Notion
     or Box link, or any `https://` URL. The file stays where it is and opens
     there, under that service's own sharing. If you've connected an account
     under **Profile**, you can browse and pick instead of pasting. Setting up
     those connections is covered in [Documents & files](/integrations/files).
   - **Upload** — choose a PDF, PNG, JPEG, WebP, Word (`.docx`), Excel
     (`.xlsx`), PowerPoint (`.pptx`), text or CSV file to keep in
     Consultainer. The file picker only offers those types. Files must be
     under 5 MB, or 25 MB when the instance stores uploads in S3-compatible
     storage.
3. Optionally give it a **Title** and a **Type**. On a client, pick a
   **Project**, or leave **Whole client**.
4. Choose **Client can see it** (see below).
5. Choose **Who on your team can see it** (see below).
6. Click **Add link** or **Upload**.

### Who can see a document

**Client can see it**

- **No — internal only** (the default). Never shown to the client.
- **Yes — on their client links.** Listed on the client's
  [share pages](#client-links), badged **Client can see** in the app. An
  uploaded file can be opened from the share page. A linked file opens where
  it lives, so the client also needs access there.

**Who on your team can see it**

- **Everyone in the organization** (the default).
- **Owners & admins.**
- **Specific people** — tick who.

Owners and whoever added the document can always see it. For everyone else, a
document they're not allowed to see doesn't appear and can't be opened.

To change either setting later, click the shield icon next to the document.
Owners, whoever added it, and admins who can see it can change these settings
or remove the document. Removing a link never touches the file itself.

### Viewing documents

Click an uploaded document to open its page in Consultainer. PDFs and images
are shown right there; other file types can't be previewed, so use
**Download**. Linked documents open in a new
tab at the provider. Every open and download, of uploads and links alike, is
recorded in the [audit log](/concepts/alerts-and-audit#audit-log).

## Client links

A client link is a read-only web page you send the client. It needs no login
and never shows internal rates, costs or comments. Only owners and admins can
create, regenerate or revoke one.

There are two kinds:

| | Project link | Client link (all projects) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where | **Links** card on the project page | **Client link (all projects)** card on the client page |
| Covers | One project | Every non-confidential project for the client |
| Shows | Hours logged (against the budget, if set), billed to date, flat fee, milestones, invoices, documents marked for the client, how to pay | Hours logged and billed to date across projects, each project and its status, invoices, documents marked for the client, how to pay |
| Tasks | Optional: turn on **Show tasks** | No |

On both, the invoice list only has sent and paid invoices (never drafts), each
with its PDF and, when [online payments](/integrations/payments) are set up, a
**Pay now** button. Opening an invoice PDF from a client link counts as the
client opening that invoice (see
[tracking opens](/concepts/invoicing#tracking-opens)). An invoice that includes
any confidential project's work is never listed.

While a client's bank payment is clearing, the invoice shows **Bank payment
processing** instead of **Pay now**. After paying, the client comes back to
the same page with a confirmation for that invoice at the top. Amounts on
both pages are in your organization's **Default currency**.

### How to share a project with the client

1. Open the project and find the **Links** card.
2. Click **Generate client link**.
3. Optional: turn on **Show tasks** to list task titles and their status. Task
   comments stay hidden unless you mark one **Share with client** (see
   [Tasks](/concepts/tasks#how-to-share-a-comment-with-the-client)).
4. Click **Copy link** and send the link to the client.

To check it worked, open the link in a private browser window: you should see
the project without signing in.

To cut off access, click **Revoke** (the link stops working at once) or
**Regenerate** (the old link stops working and you get a new one to send).

### How to share all of a client's projects

1. Open the client and find **Client link (all projects)**.
2. Click **Generate client link**, then **Copy link**.

**Regenerate** and **Revoke** work the same as for a project link.

## How to import clients and projects

1. Go to **Clients** and click **Import**.
2. Click **Download CSV template** and fill it in, one row per project (leave
   the project name blank for a client with no projects yet).
3. Choose the file under **CSV file** and click **Import**.

A client name that already exists is matched rather than duplicated, so
running the same file again is safe. Rows with problems are listed after the
import so you can fix and re-run them.

Moving from Harvest, Toggl Track or Clockify? **Settings → Import** brings
over their time entries along with the clients and projects; see
[Import and export](/concepts/import-export).
