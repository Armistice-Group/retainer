## Clients

A client is the company or person you're consulting for. Each client holds:

- **Contacts** — people at the client, with email, phone, title and role. Mark
  one as primary, and tick **receives invoices** on whoever invoice emails
  should go to.
- **Billing** — billing email and address (default to the client's own),
  default **payment terms** (or the organization's), whether they get
  **overdue reminders**, and their **payment methods** (see
  [Payments](/integrations/payments)).
- **Links** — staging URLs, shared docs, Drive folders.
- **Documents** — see below.
- **Billing cycle** and **retainers** — see [Invoicing](/concepts/invoicing).

### Documents

Attach W-9s, 1099s, contracts and other paperwork (PDF or image, up to 5MB).
Click one to view it in the app; **Download** saves it.

Each document has **Who can see it**: everyone in the organization, owners &
admins, or specific people. Owners and whoever uploaded it can always see it;
for everyone else a restricted document doesn't appear and its file can't be
opened. Owners, the uploader, and admins who can see a document can change its
access or delete it. Every view and download is recorded in the
[audit log](/concepts/alerts-and-audit#audit-log).

## Projects

A project belongs to a client and has its own team, bill rates, billing type,
[tasks](/concepts/tasks), milestones and expenses.

### Billing types

| Type | How it bills |
| --- | --- |
| **Hourly** | From logged, billable time entries at each team member's rate. |
| **Flat fee** | A single fixed amount for the whole project. |
| **Milestone-based** | Per deliverable — each milestone has its own amount and is billed once marked complete. |

A project can also override the client's **payment terms** for invoices of
just its work.

### Team & bill rates

Each person on a project has their own bill rate and currency, independent of
their rate on any other project. A time entry uses that rate unless a rate
override is set on the entry.

### Confidential projects

A confidential project is visible only to its members plus owners and admins —
useful for engagements under an NDA.

### Budgets

An hourly project can have a budget in hours. The project page shows hours
logged against it and flags when it's close to or over. Owners and admins are
[alerted](/concepts/alerts-and-audit) the first time a project reaches 80% and
100% of its budget, and when a task's hours pass its estimate (the task's
assignee is told too). Each alert can fire again if hours drop back under or
the budget grows.

## Client links

Owners and admins can give a client a read-only page — no login, no internal
rates — and regenerate or revoke it at any time:

- **Project link** (on the project page) — status, hours vs. budget, billing
  totals, milestones, invoices (with **Pay now** when online payments are set
  up) and how to pay. Turn on **Show tasks** to add the task list (titles and
  status). Comments stay internal unless marked **Share with client**; only
  those appear under their task.
- **Client link** (on the client page) — the same across every
  non-confidential project for that client.

Opening an invoice PDF from either counts as the client opening the invoice.
