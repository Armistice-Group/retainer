Consultainer runs client engagements for consultancies: clients, projects,
tasks, time, milestones, expenses and invoices in one place, self-hosted on
your own server. It connects to the tools you already use (Linear, your
calendar, Slack, QuickBooks, Stripe, Google Drive and others, AI agents)
instead of being one more app to keep updated by hand.

It's open source (Apache-2.0) and self-hosted only: no per-seat pricing, no
plan limits, no telemetry.

## Who can do what

Organizations have three roles. **Members** work on clients, projects,
tasks and their own time. **Admins** also manage settings, people, reports,
approvals and sending invoices. The **Owner**, who set the organization up,
can also change instance-wide settings. The full list is in
[Team & security](/concepts/team-and-security#roles). Setup guides in these
docs say which role each step needs.
