Consultainer runs client engagements for consultancies — clients, projects,
tasks, time, milestones, expenses, and invoices in one place, self-hosted on
your own server. It's built to plug into the tools you already work in
(Linear, your calendar, Slack, QuickBooks, AI agents) rather than be another
admin app to keep updated.

## What you can do

## How these docs are organized

- **Get started** — the [quickstart](/quickstart) and
  [self-hosting](/self-hosting) reference.
- **Core concepts** — clients and projects, tasks, time, invoicing,
  milestones, expenses, reports, and alerts.
- **Integrations** — Linear, calendars, payments (Stripe, Mercury),
  QuickBooks, and Slack/email.
- **API Reference** — the REST API for scripting or integrating Consultainer
  with something else.
- **MCP Server** — connecting an AI agent so it can read and write your
  Consultainer data directly.
